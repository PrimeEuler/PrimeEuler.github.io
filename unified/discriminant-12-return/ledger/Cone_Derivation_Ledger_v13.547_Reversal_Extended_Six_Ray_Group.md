# Cone Derivation Ledger v13.547 — Reversal-Extended Six-Ray Permutation Group

## Status
Exact finite permutation calculation, building on v13.543–v13.546.

The verified six-ray generators are
\[
r=(a\ c\ b\ d),\qquad
s=(a\ f\ d)(b\ e\ c),
\]
and cyclic-orientation reversal is
\[
\rho=(a\ b)(c\ d)(e\ f).
\]

## Direct closure
Exact permutation closure gives
\[
|\langle r,s\rangle|=24,
\qquad
|\langle r,s,\rho\rangle|=48.
\]
Moreover
\[
\rho\notin\langle r,s\rangle.
\]

Direct composition verifies
\[
\rho r=r\rho,
\qquad
\rho s=s\rho.
\]
Thus \(\rho\) centralizes the verified \(S_4\) image.

Since \(\langle r,s\rangle\cong S_4\), \(\langle\rho\rangle\cong C_2\), their intersection is trivial, and they commute,
\[
\boxed{
\langle r,s,\rho\rangle\cong S_4\times C_2
}
\]
with order
\[
\boxed{48}.
\]

## Presentation
The two generators \(r,s\) satisfy
\[
r^4=s^3=(rs)^2=1,
\]
the standard spherical triangle presentation of \(S_4\). Adjoining central reversal gives
\[
\boxed{
\langle r,s,\rho\mid
r^4=s^3=(rs)^2=\rho^2=1,
[\rho,r]=[\rho,s]=1
\rangle
\cong S_4\times C_2.
}
\]

Equivalently, the reversal-extended action is the direct product of residue relabeling and orientation reversal of the cyclic ordering.

## Cyclic-order interpretation
Under v13.545, the six rays are oriented cyclic orders of
\[
\Omega=\{1,5,7,11\}
\]
modulo cyclic rotation. The \(S_4\) factor relabels the four entries, while \(\rho\) reverses cyclic orientation. These operations commute:
\[
\rho(g\cdot[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4])
=g\cdot\rho([x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]).
\]
Therefore the 48-element group is exactly the symmetry generated by arbitrary relabeling together with an independent orientation bit.
