# Cone Derivation Ledger v13.536 — Split-Quaternion / Clifford Container

Status: exploratory exact algebra; no main-theorem promotion.

## 1. Real cone as split-quaternion determinant null cone

Use the real split-quaternion algebra `M_2(R)` with basis

\[
1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
e_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
e_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
e_3=e_1e_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then
\[
e_1^2=-1,\qquad e_2^2=e_3^2=+1,
\]
so this is the split-quaternion algebra, equivalently `Cl_{1,1}` up to convention.

Embed the cone coordinates as
\[
Q(X,Y,T)=T e_3+X e_2+Y e_1
=\begin{pmatrix}T&X+Y\\X-Y&-T\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its determinant is
\[
\det Q=X^2-Y^2-T^2.
\]
After the harmless basis relabeling/sign convention appropriate to the project cone, one obtains the signature-(2,1) null form `X^2+Y^2-T^2`; equivalently one may use the complexified coefficient `Y\mapsto iY` in this matrix model. Thus the project cone is naturally a null locus in the split/Clifford container, while the arithmetic-negation lift naturally requires its complexification.

## 2. Complexified cone operators

In the project coordinates `(X,Y,T)^T`, retain
\[
R_X=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1),\qquad
R_Y=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1),
\]
and the affine-normalized arithmetic-negation lift
\[
A=\widetilde\sigma_+=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&-1\\
0&i&0\\
-1&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Exact multiplication gives
\[
A^2=R_Y,\qquad A^4=I,\qquad [A,R_Y]=0.
\]
Thus the complexified Clifford container simultaneously contains the native real reflection `R_Y` and an order-four square root `A` of that reflection.

This resolves the apparent algebraic coexistence issue from v13.535: there is no contradiction in the larger algebra. The contradiction arose only when trying to send the same `R_Y` simultaneously to two different operators in one fixed `D_8` representation.

## 3. Two distinct D8 structures inside/under the larger container

The intrinsic transverse complex structure has
\[
J:(X,Y)\mapsto(-Y,X),\qquad C=R_Y:(X,Y)\mapsto(X,-Y),
\]
with
\[
J^4=C^2=I,\qquad CJC=J^{-1},
\]
so
\[
\langle J,C\rangle\cong D_8.
\]
Here `R_Y` is a reflection.

The arithmetic lift group generated by `A` and `R_X` has order 16 (v13.534). After quotient by the full antipode `-I_3`,
\[
\langle \bar A,\bar R_X\rangle\cong D_8,
\]
and now
\[
\bar A^2=\bar R_Y
\]
is the central half-turn of this projective `D_8`.

Therefore these are genuinely different embeddings/quotients of a larger operator algebra:

- intrinsic complex-plane `D_8`: `R_Y` is reflection/conjugation;
- projective arithmetic-lift `D_8`: `R_Y` is square of the order-four lift, hence central half-turn.

They must not be identified generator-by-generator.

## 4. Split-quaternion interpretation

The mixed unit signatures of split quaternions explain why both types of operations coexist naturally: one distinguished direction squares to `-1`, while two square to `+1`. This is structurally compatible with a cone carrying a complex quarter-turn together with real involutive reflections.

The correct claim at this stage is therefore not `the cone symmetry is quaternionic`. It is:

> The complexified split-quaternion/Clifford algebra provides a natural ambient operator algebra in which the real cone reflections and the order-four arithmetic-negation lift coexist, while their two resulting `D_8` realizations remain distinct.

## 5. Guardrail and next target

This does not yet prove a canonical Clifford intertwiner with the cyclotomic field or A3 action. In particular, v13.535's obstruction remains valid for any single generator-preserving `D_8` identification.

Next target: construct the explicit action on the split-quaternion matrix `Q(X,Y,T)`, determine whether `A`, `R_X`, `R_Y`, and the intrinsic quarter-turn `J` are realized by left multiplication, right multiplication, conjugation, or combinations thereof, and classify the finite subgroup they generate in the projective Clifford action.
