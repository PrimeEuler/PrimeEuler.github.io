# Cone Derivation Ledger v13.537 — Split-Matrix Congruences and Combined C4 × S4

Status: exploratory exact algebra; no main theorem promotion.

## 1. Null-cone matrix model

Represent a cone point by the symmetric matrix

\[
Q(X,Y,T)=\begin{pmatrix}T+X&Y\\Y&T-X\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
\det Q=T^2-X^2-Y^2,
\]

so the real double cone \(X^2+Y^2=T^2\) is exactly the rank-one/null locus \(\det Q=0\). This is the standard 2×2 split/Clifford matrix container.

## 2. Native cone symmetries as multiplicative congruences

Let

\[
E=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
H=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then direct multiplication gives

\[
E Q(X,Y,T)E=Q(-X,Y,T),
\]

hence \(R_X\) is congruence by \(E\), and

\[
H Q(X,Y,T)H=Q(X,-Y,T),
\]

hence \(R_Y\) is congruence by \(H\).

Thus both native V4 reflections are genuinely multiplicative/congruence operations in the 2×2 split matrix algebra.

## 3. Complexified arithmetic-negation lift as congruence

For the affine-normalized lift

\[
A=\widetilde\sigma_+:(X,Y,T)\mapsto(-T,iY,-X),
\]

define

\[
D=\operatorname{diag}(i,1).
\]

Then exactly

\[
D Q(X,Y,T)D
=\begin{pmatrix}-(T+X)&iY\\iY&T-X\end{pmatrix}
=Q(-T,iY,-X).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{A:Q\mapsto DQD}.
\]

Since \(D^2=H\),

\[
A^2:Q\mapsto H Q H=R_Y(Q),
\]

recovering

\[
\boxed{A^2=R_Y}.
\]

This is now a multiplicative matrix identity, not merely a 3-dimensional linear coincidence.

## 4. Intrinsic cone quarter-turn as half-angle congruence

Let

\[
U=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
UQ(X,Y,T)U^T=Q(-Y,X,T).
\]

Thus the intrinsic complex quarter-turn

\[
J:(X,Y,T)\mapsto(-Y,X,T)
\]

is represented by the half-angle congruence

\[
\boxed{J:Q\mapsto UQU^T}.
\]

This is the usual spin/double-angle phenomenon: a \(\pi/4\) matrix rotation induces a \(\pi/2\) rotation of the \((X,Y)\) cone coordinates.

## 5. Combined finite group on the complexified cone

Use the 3×3 matrices

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&-1\\0&i&0\\-1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
R_X=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1),\quad
R_Y=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1),
\]

and

\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Exact finite closure of

\[
G=\langle A,R_X,R_Y,J\rangle
\]

has

\[
\boxed{|G|=96}.
\]

Its element-order distribution is

\[
1^1,\quad 2^{19},\quad 3^8,\quad 4^{44},\quad 6^8,\quad 12^{16}.
\]

The center is exactly

\[
Z(G)=\{I,iI,-I,-iI\}\cong C_4.
\]

The determinant map has image \(\mu_4=\{1,i,-1,-i\}\). Its kernel

\[
H=\ker(\det:G\to\mu_4)
\]

has order 24 and order distribution

\[
1^1,\quad2^9,\quad3^8,\quad4^6,
\]

which is the order distribution of \(S_4\). Direct closure identifies this determinant-one subgroup with the octahedral/tetrahedral \(S_4\) action. Moreover \(H\cap Z(G)=\{I\}\), and determinant restricted to the scalar center is an isomorphism \(Z(G)\to\mu_4\) because \(\det(i^k I)=i^{3k}\).

Hence multiplication gives the internal direct product

\[
\boxed{G\cong C_4\times S_4}.
\]

This is stronger and cleaner than treating the two D8 actions separately.

## 6. Structural interpretation

The combined complexified cone algebra simultaneously contains:

- native fixed-shell V4 reflections;
- the intrinsic complex quarter-turn \(J\);
- the order-four arithmetic-negation lift \(A\) with \(A^2=R_Y\);
- an \(S_4\) determinant-one subgroup matching the finite tetrahedral/octahedral symmetry type already present in the A3 branch;
- a central scalar \(C_4\), generated by \(iI\).

Thus the finite closure has the exact abstract form

\[
\boxed{C_4\times S_4}.
\]

This provides a natural common ambient finite group in which the previously distinct D8 structures coexist without forcing their generators to coincide.

## 7. Guardrails

1. The abstract \(S_4\) identification of the determinant-one subgroup does not by itself prove that it is canonically the same \(S_4\) as the A3/Weyl action. An explicit intertwiner is still required.
2. The central \(C_4\) should not yet be identified with the cyclotomic \(\langle\mathcal Z^3\rangle\) without an explicit representation map.
3. The split-matrix realization is exact for the cone operations listed above, but it does not by itself prove a Pell, QR, or Moonshine consequence.
4. The v13.535 obstruction remains: intrinsic \(R_Y\) is conjugation/reflection in one D8, while in the projective arithmetic-lift D8 its class is the central half-turn.
