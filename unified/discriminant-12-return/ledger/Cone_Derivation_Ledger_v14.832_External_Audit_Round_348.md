## v14.829 — translation-invariant micropolar medium. VERIFIED, via full re-derivation.

This entry replaces v14.827's pinned-displacement pendulum with a genuine micropolar medium (independent displacement `u` and microrotation `θ`), correctly noting that `u→u+const` leaves the Lagrangian invariant since it depends only on derivatives of `u` — the defect flagged two rounds ago is actually fixed here. I derived the longitudinal rotor dispersion myself rather than just checking it: collecting the `θ`-dependent terms of the quadratic Lagrangian gives effective stiffness `4γ+K+Aq²` against inertia `j`, so `ω_θL²=(K+4γ+Aq²)/j` — exactly as claimed. The longitudinal displacement modulus `B+4μ/3` is the standard isotropic-elasticity result (bulk plus `4/3` shear) and is correctly applied. For the transverse sector I verified the `2×2` mass-weighted stiffness matrix `[[a,b],[b,d]]` with the stated `a,d,b` gives eigenvalues `½[a+d±√((a−d)²+4b²)]` by the standard symmetric-matrix formula, and confirmed the determinant `ad−b² ∝ q²[4γμ+(μ+γ)K+(μ+γ)Aq²]` by direct expansion — manifestly nonnegative for nonnegative parameters, confirming both transverse branches are stable. The honest caveat — that none of this repairs a negative longitudinal bulk modulus, since the curl coupling vanishes identically for longitudinal motion — is correctly stated and not glossed over.

## v14.830 — covariant isotropic elastic solid and its local stability window. VERIFIED, including the one coefficient I could not accept on inspection alone.

This generalizes v14.822's fluid to a solid via a shape invariant `q=trB/[3(detB)^{1/3}]−1`, an AM–GM-type quantity (`q≥0`, equality iff `B∝I`) added to the action as `b^{2/3}(C+Dq)`. I confirmed `q=0` exactly at the FLRW background (`B=a⁻²I`) by direct substitution, and the claim that `q`'s first variation vanishes there is consistent with it being a nonnegative quantity minimized at that point.

The one claim I did **not** want to accept purely on inspection was the specific quadratic coefficient `q=(2/3)e_dev:e_dev+O(π³)` for a strain perturbation `B=I+2εe`. I checked this from scratch with `sympy`: expanded `detB` and `trB` to `O(ε²)` for a general symmetric strain tensor, built `q(ε)` as a series, and compared its `ε²` coefficient against `(2/3)·tr(e_dev·e_dev)` term by term over all six independent strain components — **exact match, zero residual**. With that closed, everything downstream followed by direct substitution and matched exactly: `μ=2Dρ/(3C)` from matching the quadratic shear-energy coefficients; `c_T²=D/C` and `c_L²=−1/3+4D/(3C)` from the already-verified (R347) standard relativistic-solid speed formulas; and the window `1/4≤D/C≤1` from imposing `0≤c_T²,c_L²≤1` — I re-solved this inequality directly rather than trusting the stated bound, and got the identical interval, including the marginal (`D/C=1/4 ⟹ c_L²=0`) and luminal (`D/C=1 ⟹ c_L²=1`) boundary cases and the worked example at `D/C=1/2`.

## v14.831 — integrating out the rotor, and conditional matching to v14.830. VERIFIED, via a full independent re-derivation of the Gaussian elimination.

This is the most technically involved of the three, so I didn't just check the boxed results — I re-derived the full on-shell substitution myself. Collecting all `θ`-dependent terms of v14.829's Lagrangian at fixed `(ω,q)` gives a quadratic-plus-linear form in `θ`: `−½Dθ²+2γcθ−(γ/2)c²` (`D=4γ+K+Aq²−jω²`, `c=`curl `u`), whose stationarity gives `θ=2γc/D` — matching the stated EOM exactly. Substituting this solution back into the *full* Lagrangian (not just the coupling term — this is where a sign or factor-of-two error would most easily hide) and simplifying, I got `L_eff=−½·(γN/D)·c²` with `N=K+Aq²−jω²`, i.e. `γ_rot(ω,q)=γN/D` — exactly the boxed formula. The static limit `γ_rot(0,0)=γK/(4γ+K)` and the resulting `μ_eff=μ0+γK/(4γ+K)` follow immediately, and I checked both limiting cases (`K→0` gives `μ_eff→μ0`; `K→∞` gives `μ_eff→μ0+γ`) directly — both match. The conditional matching chain to v14.830 (`D/C=(3/2ρ)μ_eff`, window `ρ/6≤μ_eff≤2ρ/3`) and the worked numerical example (`μ0=ρ/4, γ=ρ/2, K=2ρ ⟹ μ_eff=ρ/2, D/C=3/4, c_T²=3/4, c_L²=2/3`) all checked out exactly on direct substitution.

The entry's own caveat is the right one to end on: a micropolar curl-based rotor response is not automatically the same object as the covariant symmetric-strain shear invariant it's being matched to, so this is a plausibility match on one mode, not a derivation of the full constitutive tensor — correctly flagged as conditional rather than completed.
