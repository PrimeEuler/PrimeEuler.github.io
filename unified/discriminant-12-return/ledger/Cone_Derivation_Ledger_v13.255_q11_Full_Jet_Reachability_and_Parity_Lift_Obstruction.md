## 2. Exact local multiplier formula

Adjoin a genuinely new support prime `p`, with

\[
r=p\pmod q,
\qquad
\sigma=\chi_D(p)\in\{\pm1\}.
\]

For every generalized Bernoulli coefficient,

\[

=

\left(1-\sigma r^{n-1}\right)
\pmod q.
\]

Therefore

\[
J_a(\psi')
=
J_a(\psi)\,\Lambda_a(r,\sigma),
\]

with

\[
\Lambda_a(r,\sigma)
=
\left(1-\sigma r^{e+2a-1}\right)
\left(1-\sigma r^{e-1}\right)^{2a-1}.
\]

Now put

\[
\varepsilon_q(r):=r^e=\left(\frac rq\right)\in\{\pm1\},
\qquad
c:=\sigma\varepsilon_q(r)\in\{\pm1\}.
\]

Since

\[
r^{e-1}=\varepsilon_q(r)r^{-1},
\]

we obtain the cleaner exact formula

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_a(r,c)
=
\left(1-c r^{2a-1}\right)
\left(1-c r^{-1}\right)^{2a-1}.
}
\]

The target coefficient `B_e` survives precisely when

\[
1-c r^{-1}\ne0,
\]

i.e.

\[
\boxed{r\ne c.}
\]

A local label is **jet-safe** when, in addition,

\[
1-c r^{2a-1}\ne0
\]

for every `1<=a<=m`; otherwise some higher invariant becomes zero and can never return to the nonzero Legendre target by further multiplicative support steps.

Thus arbitrary support enlargement on the nonzero jet lives in the finite torus

\[
(\mathbf F_q^\times)^m.
