## 1. Question

v13.241 proved that the second determinantal divisor `Delta_2` is a one-way obstruction: a q=7 support word that leaves the prototype sector can never return. The natural next question was whether the exterior orbit merely contains finitely many nonprototype classes, or whether the integral lattice content grows without bound.

The answer is stronger than a finite search: on the rank-two prototype signal plane every one of the twelve q=7 support operators has an explicit 2x2 integral matrix, and `Delta_2` is exactly the product of their determinant weights. This proves that the noncollapsed operator orbit is infinite.

## 2. Primitive rank-two signal lattice

Use the q=7 prototype from v13.239-v13.241. Its first three distinct cofactor signals are

\[
b_1=f_1=(-1,1,1,0,-1,-1,1)^T,
\]

\[
b_2=f_2=(0,1,-2,0,2,-1,0)^T,
\]

and

\[
f_3=(1,-2,1,0,-1,2,-1)^T=-b_1-b_2.
\]

By cofactor reflection,

\[
f_4=f_3,\qquad f_5=f_2,\qquad f_6=f_1.
\]

Hence, with

\[
B=[b_1\ b_2]\in M_{7\times2}(\mathbb Z),
\]

the full prototype fiber matrix factors as

\[
\boxed{
F_*=BC,
}
\]

where

\[
C=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&-1&-1&0&1\\
0&1&-1&-1&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Because the first two columns of `C` are the standard basis, the columns of `F_*` generate exactly the lattice generated by `b_1,b_2`. Directly, the gcd of the 2x2 minors of `B` is 1. Thus this is a primitive rank-two lattice and

\[
\Delta_2(F_*)=1.
\]

## 3. Exact restriction of every q=7 support operator

\[
(T_{r,\sigma}f)(k)=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}f(rk+j)-\sigma f(k).

## 4. Determinant-weight alphabet

The twelve letters therefore fall into four exact classes:

\[
\boxed{
|\det R_{r,\sigma}|=
\begin{cases}
0,&r=\pm1,\ \sigma=+1,\\
4,&r=\pm1,\ \sigma=-1,\\
3,&r\in\{2,3,4,5\},\ \sigma=+1,\\
1,&r\in\{2,3,4,5\},\ \sigma=-1.
\end{cases}
}
\]

The determinant-one letters are exactly the prototype-preserving alphabet of v13.240. The determinant-three and determinant-four letters are the two noncollapsed exterior classes of v13.241. The determinant-zero letters annihilate the prototype plane.

Thus the preserving/nonpreserving classification is not merely an empirical partition of the twelve operators: it is the unimodular/nonunimodular partition of their exact rank-two integral representation.

## 5. Exact determinant cocycle for arbitrary words

Let a support word be

\[
w=((r_1,\sigma_1),\ldots,(r_m,\sigma_m)).
\]

Its restricted matrix is

\[
R_w=R_{r_m,\sigma_m}\cdots R_{r_1,\sigma_1}.
\]

If no determinant-zero letter occurs, then every factor has rank two and

\[
\det R_w=\prod_{j=1}^m\det R_{r_j,\sigma_j}.
\]

Moreover

\[
F_w=A_wF_*=BR_wC.
\]

Because `B` is primitive of rank two and `C` contains the standard basis columns, the second determinantal divisor is exactly the absolute determinant of `R_w`:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_2(F_w)=|\det R_w|.
}
\]

Let

- `a(w)` be the number of letters with `r in {2,3,4,5}` and `sigma=+1`;
- `b(w)` be the number of letters with `r=+-1` and `sigma=-1`.

Then every noncollapsed word satisfies the exact formula

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_2(F_w)=3^{a(w)}4^{b(w)}.
}
\]

If a determinant-zero letter occurs, then the rank-two prototype plane is annihilated and all later states remain rank deficient.

This strengthens the divisibility statement of v13.241 to an exact multiplicative cocycle.

## 6. Exterior strata are arithmetically graded

The noncollapsed q=7 orbit is therefore graded by the monoid

\[
\boxed{
\{3^a4^b:a,b\ge0\}.
}
\]

The recurrent prototype component is exactly the grade

\[
(a,b)=(0,0),\qquad \Delta_2=1.
\]

A determinant-three letter moves irreversibly in the `a` direction; a determinant-four letter moves irreversibly in the `b` direction. Unimodular preserving letters may change phase inside a fixed grade but cannot change `(a,b)`.

Consequently the exterior is not an undifferentiated collection of failed resonance states. It carries an exact two-coordinate arithmetic depth.

## 7. Infinite operator orbit

Take the letter `(2,+1)`. Its restricted matrix is

\[
R_{2,+}=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det R_{2,+}=3.
\]

Therefore after `n` repetitions,

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_2\bigl(T_{2,+}^n\Phi_*\bigr)=3^n.
}
\]

These fibers are pairwise inequivalent under the v13.239 decorated-fiber equivalence because `Delta_2` is an equivalence invariant. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\text{the q=7 operator orbit of the prototype is infinite.}
}
\]

This is an exact theorem, not a bounded computational observation.

Similarly, repeated `(1,-1)` gives

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_2\bigl(T_{1,-}^n\Phi_*\bigr)=4^n.
}
\]

Thus the exterior contains at least two independent unbounded determinant directions.

## 8. Realization by genuinely new support primes

Repeated use of one local label in the operator alphabet must correspond, in the support problem, to adjoining distinct new primes carrying that same label. For `D=-8`, the label `(2,+1)` means

\[
p\equiv2\pmod7,
\qquad
\chi_{-8}(p)=+1.
\]

For odd primes, `chi_{-8}(p)=+1` when `p=1` or `3 mod 8`. CRT therefore gives admissible reduced residue classes modulo 56, for example

\[
\boxed{p\equiv9\pmod{56}}
\]

(and also `p=51 mod 56`). Since these classes are coprime to 56, Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions supplies infinitely many distinct primes in either class.

Therefore the formal infinite operator sequence can be realized by an actual infinite chain of genuinely new support-prime adjunctions. In particular, there exist support sets `S_n` with `n` newly adjoined primes, all carrying label `(2,+1)`, for which

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_2(F_{S_n})=3^n.
}
\]

So the infinite orbit is not merely an algebraic artifact of allowing a support letter to repeat abstractly.
