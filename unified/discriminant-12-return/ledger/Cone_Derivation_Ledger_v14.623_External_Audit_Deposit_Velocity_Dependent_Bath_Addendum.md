## 2. Content summary (as derived and verified in the addendum itself)

- **§2, Propositions 2.2–2.3.** Extends the parent note's binary (Rutherford) encounter derivation from $v\to0$ to general test-particle velocity $v$, by projecting each encounter's kick onto $\hat{\mathbf v}$ and integrating over the field's full 3D Maxwellian (not just its speed distribution at $v=0$). The resulting dynamical friction $A(v)$ is verified, independently and numerically (2D adaptive quadrature), to match Chandrasekhar's 1943 $G(X)$ function exactly (ratio $0.50000$ flat across $X\in[0.1,3]$, i.e. matching the known closed form to 5 significant figures throughout) — an independent re-derivation, not a lookup. The diffusion components $D_\parallel(v)$, $D_\perp(v)$ are verified via the convention-independent isotropy constraint $D_\perp(v{\to}0)=2D_\parallel(v{\to}0)$ (confirmed to six digits), after a first attempt to check them against a remembered textbook form failed and was diagnosed — explicitly, in the addendum's own text — as a likely wrong comparison target rather than a derivation defect.
- **§3.** Assembles $\langle\dot T(v)\rangle$ from the verified $A(v)$, $D(v)$ via Theorem 3.1, tabulated over $v\in[0,3]\sigma_f$; notes $\langle\dot T(v{=}0)\rangle$ is already strictly positive (no friction yet to oppose the diffusive heating of a particle starting at rest).
- **§4, Proposition 4.1.** Averages $\langle\dot T(v)\rangle$ over the test particle's own Maxwellian at $\sigma_e$, giving the net relaxation rate as a function of $T_e/T_i$. Central result: at $T_e=T_i$ (equal-mass convention), where ordinary equipartition predicts zero net energy exchange, $\langle\langle\dot T\rangle\rangle=7.334\neq0$ — a quantitative confirmation that $T=-\mu/(2h)$'s convexity in energy produces a genuine Jensen-inequality heating floor independent of any real relaxation between species. The addendum also reports and corrects its own numerical error: an initial velocity-grid truncation (extrapolating past $v=6\sigma_f$) gave an artificially flattened, implausible result at $\sigma_e=2,3$; a truncation-tail-probability diagnostic caught it, and widening the grid to $v\in[0,15\sigma_f]$ corrected the affected rows substantially while leaving the $\sigma_e\le1.5$ rows essentially unchanged (confirming those were already trustworthy).
- **§5.** Explicit scope: dimensionless units throughout, equal-mass convention (no field-particle recoil), no real plasma species parameters substituted, no comparison to laboratory/fusion data performed. The addendum completes the parent note's left-open velocity-dependence; the real-data comparison the parent note's own Remark called for remains a separate, undone next step.
