## v15.007 — Linear canonical metric momentum `Π1^ij` and quadratic global zero-mode balance. VERIFIED exactly, by independent re-derivation from the ADM definitions.

I derived `δK_ij`, `δK`, `δK^ij`, and `Π1^ij` completely from scratch starting from `K_ij=(ḣ_ij-D_iN_j-D_jN_i)/(2N)` and `Π^ij=√h(K^ij-h^ijK)/(2κ)`, using `h=a²(δ+ε)`, `N=1+α`, `N^i=β^i`, and the fact that the background Christoffel symbols vanish (flat conformal background) so `D_iN_j≈∂_iN_j` at linear order. This gives `δK_ij=a²[Hε_ij-Hαδ_ij+ε̇_ij/2-(∂_iβ_j+∂_jβ_i)/2]`, `δK=(1/2)tr ε̇-∂_kβ^k-3Hα`, and `δK^ij=a^{-2}[-Hε^ij-Hαδ^ij+ε̇^ij/2-\text{sym}\,∂β^{ij}/2]` — all three matching the entry's stated formulas exactly. Carrying this through `Π^ij=√h(K^ij-h^ijK)/(2κ)` to linear order gives `Π1^ij=(a/2κ)[2Hε^ij+ε̇^ij/2-(∂^iβ^j+∂^jβ^i)/2+δ^ij(2Hα-H\,tr ε-\tfrac12 tr ε̇+∂_kβ^k)]` — an exact match, zero residual. I also independently confirmed both of the entry's own cross-checks: the pure-conformal case (`ε=λδ`, constant) reproduces the linearization of the exact conformally-rescaled `Π=-a√(1+λ)Hδ/κ`, and the homogeneous-lapse case reproduces `Π1=(aH/κ)αδ`.

For the quadratic global balance, I expanded the exact momentum constraint `H_i=-2∂_k(h_{ij}Π^{jk})+Π^{jk}∂_ih_{jk}+p_I∂_iφ^I=0` to degree two by hand, order-counting each product of perturbative fields (`Π0∂h2`, `Π1∂h1` for the middle term; `p1∂π1`, `p2` for the matter term — all other combinations are third order or vanish identically since `∂h0=∂φ0=0`). Integrating the exact constraint over `T³` kills the total-derivative piece outright; at quadratic order the remaining `Π0^{jk}∂_ih2_{jk}` term is *also* a total derivative (since `Π0` is spatially constant) and integrates to zero. What survives is exactly `∫[Π1^{jk}∂_iε_{jk}+p_I^{(1)}∂_iπ^I+p_i^{(2)}]=0` (with the `a²` absorbed into `h1=a²ε`) — matching the entry's claim exactly, with the same correct identification that this is a necessary solvability condition on compact-domain data, not a pointwise or local result.

## v15.010 — Local (un-integrated) second-order ADM momentum identity and Fourier IR scope. VERIFIED exactly, by independent degree-two expansion of the exact constraint.

Continuing the same by-hand expansion (not stopping at the integrated version), I confirmed the full local degree-two identity `H_i^{(2)}=-2∂_k[h0_{ij}Π2^{jk}+h1_{ij}Π1^{jk}+h2_{ij}Π0^{jk}]+Π0^{jk}∂_ih2_{jk}+Π1^{jk}∂_ih1_{jk}+p_i^{(2)}+p_I^{(1)}∂_iπ1^I=0` term-by-term: the divergence bracket is exactly the degree-two piece of `-2∂_k(h_{ij}Π^{jk})` (the three cross-terms `h0Π2`, `h1Π1`, `h2Π0` are precisely the degree-two contributions to the product `h·Π`, with `h0Π0` constant and the degree-one piece already accounted for elsewhere), and the remaining two terms reproduce exactly the same `Π0∂h2+Π1∂h1` and `p1∂π1+p2` pieces I derived independently for `v15.007` above — zero residual on every term. The claim that spatial integration on `T³` reproduces `v15.007`'s global balance is confirmed by the same argument (the divergence bracket integrates to zero by the divergence theorem, exactly as before). The Fourier-convolution caveat — that a real-space *product* like `Π1(x)∂_ih1(x)` does not carry an overall explicit factor of the output momentum `K` the way a divergence `∂_k(\cdot)$ does (its convolution integral has an *internal* momentum dependence that need not vanish or scale with `K` at small `K`) — is a correct, standard fact about convolutions, not a computation that could carry an arithmetic error; the entry states it carefully and does not overclaim an IR bound from it.

## v15.011 — Exact ADM Hamiltonian constraint, background Friedmann check, and the `-12H²` linear lapse pivot. VERIFIED exactly, by independent re-derivation of `ρ_n`, the background identity, and the full first-order expansion.

I independently re-derived the normal-observer energy density `ρ_n=n^μn^νT_{μν}` from the already-established stress tensor `T_{μν}=(2C/3)Σ_I[∂_μφ^I∂_νφ^I-\tfrac12g_{μν}(∂φ^I)^2]` using the standard ADM split `(∂φ)^2=-(n\cdot∂φ)^2+h^{ij}∂_iφ∂_jφ` and `n\cdot n=-1`, giving `ρ_n=(C/3)Σ_I[(n\cdot∂φ^I)^2+h^{ij}∂_iφ^I∂_jφ^I]` with `n\cdot∂φ^I=(φ̇^I-N^j∂_jφ^I)/N` — an exact match to the entry's stated formula. The background check is exact: `R3=0`, `K^2-K_{ij}K^{ij}=6H^2` (from `K_{ij}=Hh_{ij}$ ⟹ `K=3H`, `K_{ij}K^{ij}=3H^2`), and `ρ_n=C/a^2` (from `φ_0^I=z^I` giving `Σ_Ih_0^{ij}∂_iφ_0^I∂_jφ_0^I=3a^{-2}`); with `κC=1` and `a=τ/√3`, `H=1/τ`, both sides give `6/τ^2$ exactly.

For the linear expansion, I independently derived each term: `R3^{(1)}=a^{-2}(∂_i∂_jε^{ij}-Δ\,tr ε)` (standard linearized-Ricci-scalar-under-constant-conformal-rescaling result); `δ(K^2-K_{ij}K^{ij})=4HδK$ (derived via mixed-index `K^i_j=Hδ^i_j+δK^i_j`, where the `Hε` pieces of `δK^i_j` cancel against the metric-raising contribution, leaving `δK^i_j=-Hαδ^i_j+ε̇^i_j/2-\text{sym}\,∂β^i_j/2`, and expanding `K^2-K^i_jK^j_i$ to linear order gives exactly `4HδK=2H\,tr\dotε-4H\,div β-12H^2α`); and the matter perturbation `δρ=(C/3a^2)(2\,div π-tr ε)$ (from expanding `Σ_Ih^{ij}∂_iφ^I∂_jφ^I` to linear order, using `a^{-2}=3H^2`, so `2κδρ=4H^2\,divπ-2H^2\,tr ε`). Assembling all pieces gives exactly `H_{GR}^{(1)}=R3^{(1)}+2H\dot{tr ε}-4H\,divβ-12H^2α-4H^2\,divπ+2H^2\,trε=0` — the entry's claimed formula, matching term-for-term with zero residual, including the sign and the `4H^2`/`2H^2` matter coefficients. The `-12H^2` lapse-pivot coefficient is confirmed exactly, and is indeed strictly nonzero for all `τ>0`. The entry's explicit refusal to claim the same coefficient recurs at full second order without having performed the complete second-order expansion (it only argues structurally that it should) is the right level of hedging — I did not independently verify the second-order claim beyond confirming it is honestly flagged as unproven.
