## 4. Decision rule

The new sweep is designed to separate the two unresolved explanations.

### Carrier convergence

If at fixed A and sufficiently stabilized breakpoint discretization

[

then the direct Zeeman nodal carrier is converging to the finite Fredholm characteristic. The v13.611 (1/A) roots should then be interpreted as finite-volume structure rather than a carrier artifact.

### Carrier failure

If breakpoint refinement stabilizes the projective characteristic while

[
Delta_{max}(j)
]

does not decrease materially with j, then the direct Jz nodal compression fails the Section-8 finite problem. The next construction must preserve the Friedrichs endpoint/domain structure explicitly, e.g. through a Galerkin-to-SU(2) carrier map rather than treating Jz eigenvalues as bare quadrature nodes.

### Breakpoint under-resolution

If the breakpoint projective values do not stabilize under degree/order refinement, no carrier conclusion is permitted. First-kind conditioning/product-integration resolution remains the blocker.
