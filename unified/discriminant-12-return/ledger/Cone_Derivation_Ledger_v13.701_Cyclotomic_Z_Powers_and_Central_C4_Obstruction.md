## 1. Exact powers of the cyclotomic operator

Since \(H\) commutes with \(iI\) and \(H^2=3I\),
\[
\mathcal Z^2
=\frac{H^2+2iH-I}{4}
=\frac{2I+2iH}{4}
=\boxed{\frac{I+iH}{2}}.
\]

Then
\[
\mathcal Z^3
=
\mathcal Z^2\mathcal Z
=
\frac{(I+iH)(H+iI)}{4}.
\]
Expand:
\[
(I+iH)(H+iI)
=
H+iI+iH^2+i^2H.
\]
Because \(H^2=3I\) and \(i^2=-1\),
\[
H+iI+3iI-H
=4iI.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Z^3=iI.
}
\]

Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Z^6=-I,\qquad
\mathcal Z^9=-iI,\qquad
\mathcal Z^{12}=I.
}
\]

Thus the scalar fourth roots of unity are literally the cubic-power subgroup of the cyclotomic \(C_{12}\):
\[
\boxed{

=
\{I,iI,-I,-iI\}
=
\mu_4.
}
\]

### Representation guardrail

The foundation's displayed \(\mathcal Z\) is a \(2\times2\) cyclotomic operator, whereas v13.537's \(iI\) is scalar on the \(3\)-dimensional complexified cone-coordinate representation. The identity \(\mathcal Z^3=iI\) is exact in the cyclotomic representation, and scalar \(i\) extends canonically to \(iI_3\) on the cone representation. Hence the **central scalar action** is representation-independent after scalar extension.

This does **not** identify the full \(2\times2\) operator \(\mathcal Z\) with any particular \(3\times3\) cone operator. What is identified is its cubic power / scalar \(C_4\).

## 5. Relation to the recent reflection axis

The recent intrinsic complex reflection is
\[
R_Y:z=X+iY\mapsto\bar z.
\]

On the fixed-shell complex plane, this is precisely the real-linear action of complex conjugation. Therefore
\[
R_Y(i z)=-i\,R_Y(z),
\]
or operatorially,
\[
\boxed{

This is the same dihedral relation as
\[

The relation is exact at the level of scalar phase/conjugation. It does not imply that every occurrence of \(R_Y\) in the larger \(3\times3\) linear representation is literally antilinear conjugation; the semilinear structure must be tracked.
