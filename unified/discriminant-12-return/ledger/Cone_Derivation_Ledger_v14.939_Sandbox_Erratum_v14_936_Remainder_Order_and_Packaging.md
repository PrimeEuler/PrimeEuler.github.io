## 1. Remainder order: o(1/ln N) downgraded to O(1/ln N)

v14.936 §3 concluded: "N·(R(N) − CT(N)) = o(1/ln N) at the audited asymptotic level." That overstates what the return's own remainder envelope proves. The envelope is |R_CT(N)| ≤ C_R/ln N + C_sp/N + |γ_E| with C_R ≈ 15 inherited from the audited seed expansion and |γ_E| ≤ 2e-4 carried as a **flat, nonvanishing slack** on the 1/ln coefficient (D = −γ is pinned only up to that slack). A bound of this shape establishes **O(1/ln N)** and nothing smaller: the certified decay does not improve beyond 1/ln N.

Corrected claim: **N·(R(N) − CT(N)) = O(1/ln N)** in the audited block-mean sense. The measured block-anchor deviations (≈ 1e-3, decaying across the six centers) remain numerical evidence consistent with a smaller true remainder, but they are not an asymptotic certificate, and no o(·) statement about the constructed family's remainder is made. The same downgrade applies to the corresponding phrasings in the payload: DERIVATION.md §10 ("consistent with the o(1/ln N) remainder") and CERTIFICATE.md (same phrase). DERIVATION.md §0's sentence quotes v14.909 §2's template-level formulation, which is Lane A's own arithmetic with the stored exact constants; this erratum scopes the downgrade to the sandbox return's certified remainder for the constructed family. The payload files' bytes are frozen by the v14.937 / R365 hash freeze and are not altered; this entry is the record correction.

Nothing else in v14.936 changes: the drift and constant identities are exact as stated, the 1/ln coefficient statement was already made only up to the γ_E slack, all twelve anchors and their envelope are unaffected, and the obstruction (§5 of v14.936) is untouched — R365 independently checked it as pure algebra and found it sound.
