[O] Physical SM Higgs is an excitation of electroweak scalar doublet; a rotational singlet of two spin representations has neither a proven local scalar kinetic propagator nor Higgs gauge charges, observed mass, vev, or interaction strengths. An allowed real singlet scalar extension can be written but requires new field and couplings. The needed test is a constructed local composite seat operator with a physical pole and appropriate gauge-invariant couplings; none shown.
