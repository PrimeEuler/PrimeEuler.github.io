## Claim

The phase-angle integration — the (Et − px)/ħ accumulation that was the cone's first proven dynamical mechanism — does not yield a position-space force law, because its closed-orbit output is a *readout* of whichever trajectory family is fed into it. Demonstrated with a single machine on two families: the cone's own parabolas return the constant-force fingerprint exactly, and Kepler ellipses return the Kepler fingerprint exactly. The integration's recorded operations are all of this law-consuming kind: quantization of a given family, energy bookkeeping along its motions, and manufacture of position-space structure (harmonics/lobes) from its orbits. A law would have to *select* the family; the phase integral presupposes it.

## Chain of results (`phase_integration_law_test.py`)

1. **The cone's own family (the parabolas / constant force).** The closed-orbit phase integral for the bouncer (unit mass and field), J(E) = 2∫₀^{E/a} √(2m(E − ax)) dx, evaluated numerically at E = 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 gives J = 0.66667, 1.8856, 5.3333, 15.085, 42.667 — log-log slope d log J / d log E = **1.5000**, the analytic (4√2/(3a√m)) E^{3/2} of v15.469 (J(1) = 4√2/3 = 1.8856 ✓). Read as a law statement: E ~ J^{2/3}, whose quantization is the Airy comb — the fingerprint of the *uniform field*, precisely the law of which the mesh parabolas are the trajectories. The phase integration faithfully returns it — because it was fed it.
2. **The Kepler family (the imported law's trajectories).** The radial action for the Kepler effective potential (k = L = m = 1) plus the azimuthal action 2πL, at E = −0.40, −0.25, −0.15, −0.08: J_tot = 7.0248, 8.8858, 11.471, 15.708 — log-log slope d log J_tot / d log(−E) = **−0.5000**, the Delaunay fingerprint E ~ −J^{−2}. Same machine, opposite signature: the output tracks the input family exactly, with no residue pointing from one family toward the other.
3. **The pattern across the record.** Every recorded use of the phase-angle integration has this direction: the winding quantizes motions already given (v14.620); the antisymmetric channel's bookkeeping collects the Larmor loss of an imported radiation law (v15.275, v15.470 for the coefficients); phase-weighted integration over a *given* Larmor orbit reproduces the orbital lobes/harmonics exactly (2026-10-21 tests — position-space structure, explicitly an identity with the standard vector model, not a new law); the bouncer comb quantizes the uniform field (v15.469). In no instance does the integration output a trajectory family it was not fed. The eccentricity freedom Jeremy noted for the phase point (2026-10-22: the phase angle can ride any eccentricity on the cone) parametrizes orbits *within* a family — which orbit, not which law.

## Negatives / scope limits, stated plainly

It does not address the distinct eikonal/Hamilton–Jacobi inversion — reading a potential off a *complete* phase field S(x, t) — which recovers a law from a full solution by construction and presupposes the dynamics that generated the field; the cone's phase fields are its parabolas' (the uniform field's), so that route returns the same family as §1. The two fingerprints are computed at probe precision with the standard action definitions (J = ∮p dx, azimuthal action 2πL); the slopes are exact to the four digits the verdict needs. Nothing here diminishes the phase mechanism's standing as the cone's first dynamics or the results built on it (v15.469, v15.275, v15.470 stand as filed).
