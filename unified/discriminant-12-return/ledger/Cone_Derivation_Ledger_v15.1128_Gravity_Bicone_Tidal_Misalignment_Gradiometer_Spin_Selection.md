## 2. Gate 1030 — mechanical misalignment torque, exact twofold angular response

**U_tide=(g ΔQ/2)cos2θ**, **τ_tide=−∂θU=g ΔQ sin2θ**.
A pre-existing 45° misalignment produces the maximum *additive* torque magnitude |gΔQ|, whereas at exact alignment θ=0 the linear additive torque vanishes and the tidal field instead changes the angular curvature/stiffness. The sign of g depends on the potential/curvature convention and target polarization; the readout measures the product with calibrated orientation. An isotropic mass tensor ΔQ=0 gives zero torque irrespective of spin algebra. ## 4. Gate 1032 — rank-two quantum selection does NOT automatically read out singlet/T0

**Q_spin=J_Ax J_Bx−J_Ay J_By=(J_A+ J_B++J_A− J_B−)/2** (ℏ=1).
In the ordered |++>,|+−>,|−+>,|−−> basis, exact Pauli algebra gives
**Q_spin = (|++><−−|+|−−><++|)/2**.
Thus Q_spin annihilates both the diagonal singlet |S>=(|+−>−|−+>)/sqrt2 and triplet |T0>=(|+−>+|−+>)/sqrt2; it couples the **m=+1 and m=−1 triplet extremes** instead.
