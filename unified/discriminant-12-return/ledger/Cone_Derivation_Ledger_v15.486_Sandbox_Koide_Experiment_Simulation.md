1. **The prediction, restated and the anchor updated.** The roots are functions of m_e and m_μ only and are unchanged (recomputed: m^(Q) = 1776.96903, m^(δ) = 1776.96651; joint window [1776.96651, 1776.96903] MeV). The experimental situation as of the current literature (STCF overview, arXiv:2601.20423, summarizing the field; FCC-ee τ prospects, TAU2025): BESIII 2014 measured 1776.91 ± 0.12 (+0.10/−0.13); Belle II 2023 measured 1777.09 ± 0.08 ± 0.11, the most precise single to date; KEDR 2023 measured 1776.69 (+0.17/−0.19 ± 0.15); BES 1996 measured 1776.96; the HFLAV 2023 average is 1776.94 ± 0.09 and the PDG 2024 world average including Belle II is quoted in the FCC-ee prospects table as 1776.93 ± 0.09. Tensions against the window (distance/σ, total errors combined in quadrature with asymmetric errors approximated symmetrically): BES 1996: 0.02σ; BESIII: 0.33σ; world average: 0.41σ; Belle II: 0.89σ; KEDR: 1.15σ; the older value used at v15.479: 0.89σ. The singles straddle the window from both sides; the average sits 0.038 MeV below its lower edge.
2. **The decision table (Monte Carlo).** Test statistic: a future measurement is a draw m ~ N(truth, σ); z = (distance from m to the window)/σ; "survives" z ≤ 2, "dead" z ≥ 5 (Q and δ are monotone in m_τ across this range, so the m_τ-space test is the joint test). Candidate truths: the five measured centrals/average above and the window center. Precisions σ ∈ {0.09 (today's average), 0.05, 0.03, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005} MeV. Headline rows (survival probability / death probability / median z):
   - truth = root (1776.968): survives 96–98% at *every* σ — planned machines (FCC-ee: stat 0.002, syst ≈ 0.02; STCF threshold scans, same few×0.01 class) confirm decisively if the fingerprint is true.
   - truth = today's average (1776.93): at σ = 0.02, survival 57%, death ~0%, median z 1.8 — *uncomfortable, not dead*; at σ = 0.01, survival 5%, median z 3.7 (gray zone); at σ = 0.005, death 99%, median z 7.3. Five-sigma separation of this truth from the root requires σ ≈ 0.0076 MeV.
   - truth = 1776.86 (the v15.479 anchor): σ = 0.02 gives median z 5.3, death 63% — the filed kill estimate, confirmed for that anchor; σ ≤ 0.01: death ~100%.
   - truth = Belle II's 1777.09: death 85% at σ = 0.02, ~100% at 0.01. Truth = KEDR's 1776.69: death ~100% already at σ = 0.03.
3. **What this does to the kill condition.** The v15.479 condition — decidable at σ ≈ 0.02–0.03 — was computed against the then-current central 1776.86 (distance to window 0.107 MeV). Against the present average the distance is 0.038 MeV, so the same σ buys proportionally less: the realistic deciding paths are (a) a σ ≈ 0.005–0.01 measurement (below the FCC-ee systematic estimate of 0.02, though its 0.002 statistics leave the question to systematics control — beam-energy knowledge, as in Belle II's leading systematic), or (b) the central value's trajectory across BESIII/Belle II full-dataset/STCF generations converging to one side of the window at the σ ≈ 0.02 level several times over. The prediction itself, its coincidence grade, and its selection-effect disclosure (v15.479) are untouched: this entry updates the anchor and the decision geometry only.

## Negatives / scope limits, stated plainly

A simulation of the decision is not a simulation of the outcome: nothing here is evidence for or against the fingerprint beyond the cited measurements themselves, and the anchor update (the average's move toward the window) is a 0.07 MeV shift of a quantity with σ = 0.09–0.12 — a nudge, not a trend established. The tensions use symmetrized total errors; the Monte Carlo assumes Gaussian measurements with the stated σ and no systematic floor correlations between generations. Facility precision figures are design/projection values from the cited prospects documents, not guarantees. The fingerprint remains coincidence-grade with its selection effects as filed at v15.479; this entry neither raises nor lowers that grade.
