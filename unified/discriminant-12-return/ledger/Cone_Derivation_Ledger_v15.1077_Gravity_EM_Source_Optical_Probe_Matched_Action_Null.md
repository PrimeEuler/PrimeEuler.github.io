## Gate 889 — source is not probe
The Maxwell electromagnetic Lorentz force density is f^mu=F^{mu nu}J_nu when charges/currents are present; source-free cavity interior has J=0 and f=0. Nevertheless its EM stress tensor is nonzero and covariantly conserved in the test-field approximation (physical global apparatus requires wall stress). In Einstein gravity, T_EM couples to curvature through the standard gravitational field equation with a gravitational coupling, not through the Lorentz force. A distinct EM probe (laser/interferometer, resonator or atomic clock) measures the resulting metric/curvature; a strain or phase readout is not a direct identification of a novel gravitational force law.

## Gate 890 — exact idealized optical phase transfer
On an ideal fixed-coordinate-mirror, equal-arm interferometer transverse to propagation of a weak plus TT wave, use in c=1 units ds²=-dt²+(1+h(t))dx²+(1-h(t))dy²+dz² (ignore z-dependence at the beam-splitter plane; wave fronts transverse to both arms). At O(h) a null ray obeys dt=(1±h/2)d(distance) in the two arm directions. After one out-and-back ray in each arm, including the retarded sampling of h along both legs and neglecting O(h²) endpoint/path corrections, the **difference** in roundtrip coordinate times, equal to beam splitter proper time at this order in TT gauge, is
ΔT(t0)=∫_{t0}^{t0+2L} h(t) dt.
For h=h0 cos(Omega t), exact first-order integration gives
ΔT=(2h0/Omega) sin(Omega L) cos[Omega(t0+L)].
The Omega->0 limit is ΔT=2L h0. A monochromatic optical carrier with angular frequency omega_laser has first-order relative phase Δphi=omega_laser ΔT. The response has transfer zeros when Omega L is a nonzero integer multiple of pi, entirely due to conventional interferometer light-travel averaging, and not new arithmetic Cone nodes. This is an *ideal GR comparator*: mirrors, readout control, frequency locking, material stresses and fully gauge-invariant finite-detector treatment need independent modeling for a real experiment.

## Gate 891 — Maxwell field amplitude, parity and matched-null test
The v15.1076 standing-wave stress T_EM is quadratic in electromagnetic field amplitude E0. With a fixed retarded gravitational response kernel and fixed source geometry, linearized gravitational perturbation h is proportional to T_EM, hence to E0². Therefore an independent weak optical probe phase Delta phi scales E0². Reversing E0->-E0 changes no stress or metric response; E0->2E0 multiplies the leading phase by four. These are standard source-power predictions conditional on negligible backreaction/perturbative field regime and an interferometer geometry actually sensitive to the sourced gravitational mode. A source's instantaneous Lorentz force on a charged probe, by contrast, scales linearly in EM field amplitude and depends on charge; separating force pickup is essential in a real measurement. The measurement does not automatically isolate a tiny gravitational response from ordinary EM leakage or mechanical effects.
