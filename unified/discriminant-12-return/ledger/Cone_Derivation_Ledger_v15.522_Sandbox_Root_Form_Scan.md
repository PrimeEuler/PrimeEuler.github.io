## Claim

For α⁻¹(0) = 137.035999177 ± 0.000000021 (CODATA 2022; the same coupling as Door III's α₁ at zero momentum, and the most precisely known real in the door's family): no pure root, no small rational or root-of-rational form, no π-form, and no integer polynomial relation of degree ≤ 3 with coefficients ≤ 10⁶ fits. For sin²θ_W the best post-hoc approximants sit exactly at the scan's look-elsewhere expectation (chance grade). For α_s(m_Z) and α⁻¹(m_Z) the measurement precision is too coarse for any family to discriminate — filed as untestable, not as evidence for the hypothesis. Door III therefore stands as measured at v15.429, with the root exit specifically closed at small heights; the unbounded version of the hypothesis (arbitrary integers, arbitrary degree) is unfalsifiable by density and carries no information.

## Negatives / scope limits, stated plainly

The bounds are the claim: denominators and PSLQ coefficients ≤ 10⁶, polynomial degree ≤ 3, π-powers ≤ 2, four targets. A root form outside those bounds is not excluded — it is also not a hypothesis with content until someone names it in advance, since algebraic numbers are dense and an unbounded search fits anything (the same selection-effect discipline as v15.479). α(0) is not literally one of Door III's three values (those live at m_Z in a scheme); it is the same coupling at zero momentum and the only member of the family measured at root-killing precision — the m_Z values' imprecision is itself part of the result (§6). The record's extraction (§0) is untouched by this scan, in both directions: it neither supports a root form nor is threatened by the negative.
