**R_eta Z^ell_{g,m}=(1/2)sqrt(ell+g+1/2) Y^ell_{g+1/2,m}**.

## Gate 161 — integer vs half-integer SU(2) sectors require a spinorial carrier

A linear operator commuting with ordinary SU(2) must vanish between these opposite central characters.
