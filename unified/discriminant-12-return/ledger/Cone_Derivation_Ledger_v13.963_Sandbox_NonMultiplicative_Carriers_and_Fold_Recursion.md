## 1. Track 1: non-multiplicative carriers [N]

### 1.1 Motivation

v13.948 crowned "multiplicativity + seed-generation" as the universal
carrier (μ and λ both R≈3.77 despite different sign structure and
support). But both test functions were multiplicative. The additive
functions ω(n) = #{p|n} (distinct) and Ω(n) = #{p^k|n} (with multiplicity)
are generated by the same 168-prime seed, yet are *not* multiplicative
(they are additive: ω(mn)=ω(m)+ω(n) for coprime m,n). If they carry the
spectrum, the carrier broadens past multiplicativity. If null, the
v13.948 claim sharpens.

### 1.2 Method [D/N]

ω, Ω computed to N=10⁶ and N=10⁷ by linear sieve (exact arithmetic [D]).
Both have mean ~log log n, so an explicit log-log trend was subtracted
before the pipeline's usual uniform-filter detrending; the identical
log-FFT R statistic as v13.942/v13.948 was then applied [N]. Control: ω
with values randomly permuted (same marginal distribution, arithmetic
destroyed).

### 1.3 Results [N]

| function      | R (N=10⁶) | R (N=10⁷) | note                    |
|---------------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| ω(n)          | 1.180     | 1.143     | stable                  |
| Ω(n)          | 1.213     | 1.244     | stable                  |
| scrambled ω   | 0.947     | —         | null control            |
| μ(n) (v13.948)| 3.776     | —         | multiplicative          |
| λ(n) (v13.948)| 3.761     | —         | multiplicative          |

### 1.4 Interpretation [I]

The additive functions are **weak but real carriers**: R≈1.2, stable
across a 10× scale-up, cleanly above the distribution-matched scrambled
null (0.95). They do not wash out like v13.952's twist did — the signal,
though weak, is robust.

But compare the strengths: 3.77 (multiplicative) vs 1.2 (additive). The
refinement of v13.948's "universal carrier" is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{multiplicativity is a }3\times\text{ amplifier, not a strict gate.}
}
\]

Seed-generation + ζ in the Dirichlet series suffices for *some* carrier
(ω's Dirichlet series is ζ(s)·Σ_p p^{-s}, so ζ's zeros are present as
zeros rather than poles — consistent with a weaker but nonzero signal);
multiplicativity promotes it to the strong R≈3.77 regime. Additive
functions whisper; multiplicative functions shout. The v13.948 claim
stands, sharpened: the *strong* universal carrier is multiplicative.

## 2. Track 2: fold-recursion scale invariance [N]

### 2.1 Motivation

v13.944 established the exact scale matching N_a=e^{2a}, √N_a=e^a but left
open the recursion: does the spectral strength at scale N relate to that
at √N? The sieve backbone is exact — the seed primes ≤√N *determine* the
full pattern on [2,N] (v13.942 [D]) — so Jeremy's prediction was that the
spectrum, too, should be enclosed: R(N) scale-free.

### 2.2 Results [N]

Composite-indicator R (v13.942's object and pipeline), full [2,N]:

| N    | 10³   | 10⁴   | 10⁵   | 10⁶   | 10⁷   |
|------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| R    | 1.873 | 2.006 | 2.244 | 2.165 | 2.194 |

### 2.3 Interpretation [I]

**Confirmed, as predicted.** R is flat across four orders of magnitude
(1.87–2.24, no trend). The zero spectrum's *strength* is scale-free,
just as v13.942 found its *positions* scale-free (±0.06 across
10⁴–10⁷). The fold encloses spectrally: the seed at √N already contains
the full spectral weight measured at N. In RG language, the sieve flow
sits at a fixed point in the (R, peak-position) observables — the
instrument reads the same spectrum at every scale because there is only
one fold, recursively enclosed.
