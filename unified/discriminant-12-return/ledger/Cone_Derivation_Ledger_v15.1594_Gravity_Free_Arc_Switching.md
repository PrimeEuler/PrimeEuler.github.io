## Switching field (1559)

F is C¹ for bounded E (kernel first derivative integrals continuous); F'' is bounded weak derivative. Define G=F''-lambda p E. Then G'=(lambda p'-i)E, so G has Lipschitz representative. On free arc F=0, G=-lambda p E. At interior switching point c, provided one-sided essential traces exist, F'' on saturated side tends to lambda p(c)(Eout-Ein). Both F(c),F'(c)=0. ## Continuous switching obstruction (1560)
Assume lambda>0 and a saturated left arc ending at c<1/2 adjoining a free right arc, and suppose continuous essential control matching E(t)->e_c on both sides, |e_c|=1. Then G(c)=-lambda p(c)e_c. On saturated side, Re(conj(e_c)E(t))<=1; G(t)=G(c)+(lambda p'(c)-i)e_c(t-c)+o(|t-c|). Consequently Re(conj(e_c)F''(t))<=2lambda p'(c)(t-c)+o(|t-c|)<0 a.e. sufficiently near c from left. Since F(c)=F'(c)=0, double integration implies Re(conj(e_c)F(t))<0, contradicting F=mu E, mu>=0 and Re(conj(e_c)E)>0 near c. Same proof at c>1/2 for free left and saturated right (p'<0 and t-c>0). Thus such transitions cannot be continuous if they exist. This does not establish global arc topology, traces, magnitude or phase of jump, uniqueness, or behavior at c=1/2. No assumption of smooth phase derivative needed.
