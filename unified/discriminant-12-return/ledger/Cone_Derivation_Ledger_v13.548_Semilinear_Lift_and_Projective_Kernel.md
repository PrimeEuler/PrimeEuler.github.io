# Cone Derivation Ledger v13.548 — Semilinear Lift and Projective Kernel

## Scope
Lift the verified 48-element six-ray permutation group from v13.547 to explicit semilinear operators on the complexified cone and determine the scalar/projective kernel.

## 1. Linear cone group
Use the previously verified generators

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&-1\\0&i&0\\-1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
R_X=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1),\quad
R_Y=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1),\quad
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Exact closure gives the linear group

\[
G_{\rm lin}=\langle A,R_X,R_Y,J\rangle,
\qquad |G_{\rm lin}|=96.
\]

As established previously,

\[
G_{\rm lin}\cong C_4\times S_4,
\]

with scalar subgroup

\[
\mu_4=\{I,iI,-I,-iI\}\cong C_4.
\]

The determinant-one subgroup

\[
H=\ker(\det:G_{\rm lin}\to\mu_4)
\]

has order 24 and is the verified \(S_4\) factor.

## 2. Semilinear reversal
Let \(\kappa\) denote coordinatewise complex conjugation and

\[
K=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1).
\]

The cyclic-orientation reversal from v13.546 is

\[
\mathcal R=K\kappa,
\qquad
\mathcal R(X,Y,T)=(\bar X,\bar Y,-\bar T).
\]

It acts on the six null rays by

\[
\rho=(a\ b)(c\ d)(e\ f).
\]

Represent a semilinear operator as a pair \((M,\epsilon)\), \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\), acting by

\[
(M,\epsilon)v=M\,\overline{v}^{\,\epsilon}.
\]

Composition is

\[
(M,\epsilon)(N,\delta)
=
\bigl(M\,\overline N^{\,\epsilon},\epsilon+\delta\bmod2\bigr).
\]

Thus \(\mathcal R=(K,1)\).

## 3. Exact semilinear closure
Exact finite closure of

\[
\widetilde G
=
\langle (A,0),(R_X,0),(R_Y,0),(J,0),(K,1)\rangle
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{|\widetilde G|=192}.
\]

Its induced projective permutation action on

\[
\mathcal N_6=\{a,b,c,d,e,f\}
\]

has exactly 48 distinct permutations, agreeing with v13.547:

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{im}(\widetilde G\to S_6)
\cong S_4\times C_2.}
\]

Every one of the 48 permutations has exactly four semilinear lifts in \(\widetilde G\).

## 4. Scalar/projective kernel
The elements fixing every one of the six projective rays were computed explicitly. The kernel is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\ker(\widetilde G\to\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal N_6))
=
\{I,iI,-I,-iI\}
=
\mu_4\cong C_4.
}
\]

All four kernel elements are linear; there is no additional anti-linear element in the projective kernel.

Hence there is an exact sequence

\[
\boxed{
1\longrightarrow C_4
\longrightarrow \widetilde G
\longrightarrow S_4\times C_2
\longrightarrow1.
}
\]

and

\[
192/4=48.
\]

## 5. Reversal acts nontrivially on the scalar C4
Although reversal is central in the 48-element permutation quotient, it is not central upstairs. For \(z\in\mu_4\), semilinearity gives

\[
\mathcal R(zI)\mathcal R^{-1}=\bar z I.
\]

In particular,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R(iI)\mathcal R^{-1}=-iI.
}
\]

Thus the subgroup generated by \(iI\) and \(\mathcal R\) has presentation

\[
\langle u,\mathcal R\mid
u^4=\mathcal R^2=1,
\mathcal R u\mathcal R=u^{-1}\rangle
\cong D_8,
\]

where \(u=iI\). (The first relation is \(u^4=1\); the displayed `nu` is only a typography warning if rendered by a processor that consumes backslashes.)

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{\langle iI,\mathcal R\rangle\cong D_8.}
\]

## 6. The S4 factor commutes with reversal
Direct computation on every element of the determinant-one subgroup \(H\) gives

\[
K\,\overline h\,K=h
\qquad(h\in H).
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{\mathcal R h\mathcal R^{-1}=h\quad(h\in H).}
\]

Therefore the order-24 \(S_4\) factor commutes with the full dihedral subgroup \(\langle iI,\mathcal R\rangle\cong D_8\), and their intersection is trivial. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde G\cong S_4\times D_8,
\qquad |\widetilde G|=24\cdot8=192.
}
\]

This is stronger than merely identifying the 48-element projective quotient.

## 7. Projectivization
The central rotation subgroup of the \(D_8\) factor is

\[
\langle iI\rangle\cong C_4.
\]

Projectivizing kills this entire scalar subgroup, leaving

\[
D_8/C_4\cong C_2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde G/\mu_4
\cong
S_4\times C_2,
}
\]

exactly the 48-element six-ray permutation group.

The resulting structural diagram is

\[
\boxed{
S_4\times D_8
\xrightarrow{/\,C_4}
S_4\times C_2,
}
\]

where the quotient \(C_2\) is cyclic-orientation reversal.

## 8. Interpretation
The earlier central \(C_4\) does not remain central after adjoining anti-linear reversal. Instead reversal inverts the quarter-turn scalar:

\[
i\leftrightarrow-i.
\]

So the natural upstairs completion is not \(C_4\times S_4\times C_2\), but

\[
\boxed{S_4\times D_8.}
\]

This gives an exact realization of a dihedral structure from the interaction between the complex scalar quarter-turn and cyclic-orientation reversal.

## Guardrails
1. This is an exact finite semilinear cone-group computation and six-ray projective action.
2. The \(D_8\) factor here is generated by scalar \(iI\) and semilinear reversal \(\mathcal R\). It must not automatically be identified generator-for-generator with the other D8 structures previously found.
3. The six-ray action remains the \(S_4/C_4\) cyclic-order action, not the tetrahedral-edge action.
4. No Pell, QR, Suzuki, or Moonshine consequence is asserted by this group identification alone.
