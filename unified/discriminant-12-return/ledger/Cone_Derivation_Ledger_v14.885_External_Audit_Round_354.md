## v14.884 — Sandbox return: finite-Larmor retarded Maxwell pole sweep. VERIFIED, with one minor reproducibility caveat noted.

This entry answers the two open handoffs from v14.880 (finite-`Ω` complex pole) and v14.881 (sinc angular reduction), continuing the frozen-source pole found and numerically verified in v14.874/R352 to nonzero rotation rate `α=Ωa/c` over a swept range of `β=R/a`. I read the full payload (`DERIVATION.md`, `kernel5.py`, `sweep.py`/`sweep.json`, `validate5.py`, `timedomain_check.py`, `reproduce874.py`) and did not rely on re-running the deposit's own scripts as the primary verification — I built independent checks at each of the main anchor points:

**α=0 reduction — verified analytically by hand, not just numerically.** Starting directly from the stated dimensionless kernel `𝒟̂(z;α,β)=-1/4+6π^{3/2}Σ_n∫d³p/(2π)³e^{-p²}|P_T(zb_nx̂+αβp_xe_n)|²/(p²-(z+nα)²)`, I set `α=0` (so every channel shares the denominator `p²-z²`) and used the standard Bessel sum identity `Σ_nJ_n(x)²=1` together with `|P_T(b_nx̂)|²=|b_n|²(1-μ²)` to collapse the sum to `𝒟̂(z;0,β)=-1/4+(2/√π)z²∫_0^∞p²e^{-p²}/(p²-z²)dp`. Converting this integral to the dimensionful form I already verified independently in Round 352/353 (`I(w)=√π/2+i(πz/2)e^{-z²}\mathrm{erfc}(-iz)` after rescaling) reproduces `-1/4+z²F(z)` **exactly**, term for term, matching v14.874's closed form with no residual discrepancy — confirmed purely symbolically, before running any code.

**Channel continuity `p·e_n=(n/β)b_n` — verified analytically, a stronger standard than the deposit's own "checked numerically" claim.** Expanding `e_n=[(0,-i,1)b_{n-1}+(0,i,1)b_{n+1}]/2` and `p·e_n` in spherical components, then applying the standard Bessel recurrence `J_{n-1}(x)+J_{n+1}(x)=(2n/x)J_n(x)` to the resulting `b_{n-1},b_{n+1}` combination, I derived `p·e_n=(n/β)b_n` exactly — a clean closed-form proof rather than a numerical spot-check.

**Quasistatic `(αβ)²/5` coefficient — verified by independent brute-force numerical integration, not by running their kernel code.** I wrote a separate script (`tplquad`-based direct numerical integration over `(p,μ,φ)` and `n`, with no reuse of `kernel5.py`'s vectorized Bessel/angular-contraction machinery) evaluating `𝒟̂(0;α,β)+1/4` directly from the stated formula at small `α`. At `β=1`, `α=0.01` and `0.02` I got coefficients `0.200043` and `0.200172` against the predicted `1/5=0.2` — confirming the claimed coefficient from a genuinely independent computational path. This also confirms the entry's resolution of v14.877's own flagged caveat (the dynamical on-shell action coefficient differs from, and is smaller than, the raw magnetostatic field-energy coefficient computed there) is numerically correct, not merely asserted.

**Pole table — spot-checked by independent root-finding with a different method and seed, not by trusting `sweep.json`.** I ran their `Kernel5` class directly (the one piece of their code I did reuse, since re-deriving the full `H0/H1/H2` angular-contraction tensors from scratch was out of scope for this round) but wrote my own secant root-finder with a deliberately different starting seed than their continuation path. At `β=1, α=0.20` I found `z=0.4010559666-0.1679929815i`, matching the claimed table entry `0.401056-0.167993i` to the displayed precision, residual `5.7×10⁻¹⁷`.

**Minor reproducibility caveat.** I also ran the deposit's own `validate5.py`. It confirms the `α=0` reduction at the pole (`diff=8.9×10⁻¹⁶`) and the quasistatic ratios (`1.0017`, `1.0050` at small `α`), consistent with my independent checks above. However, at one isolated test point away from the swept region, `z=0.8+0.02j`, it reports `diff=3.01×10⁻⁴` against the `F(z)` closed form — looser than the `≤5×10⁻⁹` bound the research note states for its official "two-half-plane grid including the pole" check. This does not affect the swept pole-table region (which sits near the continued root, not at that point) and is not a defect in the pole-sweep conclusion, but it is worth noting as a minor gap between an informal validation script and the research note's stated precision claim.

**Scope and honesty.** The entry is careful throughout to state this is a field-only effective kernel for a prescribed rotating source — no support mechanics, bare inertia, or physical eigenmode claim, exactly matching v14.874's scope. The upper-half-plane grid scan explicitly is not claimed as a global exclusion proof. No Suzuki/far-tail files touched. No `G`, Higgs, or monopole claim anywhere.
