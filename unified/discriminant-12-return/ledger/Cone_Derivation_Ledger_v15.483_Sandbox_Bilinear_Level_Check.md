## Chain of results (`bilinear_check.py`)

1. **Exact decomposition in pair coordinates.** With T = (N+M)/2, X = (N−M)/2, Y = √(NM) for a same-parity pair N ≠ M: since N² − M² = 4XT and the seed's constant part contributes (π/2)(M − N) = −πX, the frozen kernel K_{N,M} = c(M z_N − N z_M)/(N²−M²) + 2σ p_N p_M (c = 2/π) is identically
   K = −1/(2T) + 2σ p_N p_M + c·(M·osc_N − N·osc_M)/(4XT),
   where osc is the seed's oscillatory part. Verified numerically on random same-parity pairs: max deviation 2.2×10⁻¹⁶. (The seed's unspecified O(1/m) part contributes a term of the form −c·C/Y² — it merges into the pole family of §2 and cannot manufacture residue structure, being smooth in each mode.)
2. **Anatomy: two exact geometric terms, one dominant band term.** (i) The drift −1/(2T) is a pure function of the shell/AM coordinate. (ii) The pole term is a geometric-mean law: p_N p_M / (L²/Y²) → 1 on adjacent pairs (measured range 0.985–1.000 across both sectors), with amplitude 2σL² = **+4.123** (even sector) vs **−0.880** (odd sector) — at the pair level the sectors differ in sign *and* magnitude, parity remaining the loudest label in the operator (cf. v15.482 §2). (iii) Both smooth terms are subdominant at range to the band term c(M·osc_N − N·osc_M)/(4XT): its RMS at fixed X is T-independent (0.413 at X = 1, 0.199 at X = 2, 0.092 at X = 4, 0.028 at X = 16, 0.013 at X = 32, in T-bands [60, 240) and [400, 900) alike), decaying ~1/X — the Hilbert-kernel profile (the action's h = 1/(N−M) = 1/(2X) in pair dress) — while the drift falls as 1/(2T).
