## Claim

The selector for the reading level exists, is internal to the machinery, and is a theorem of finite arithmetic: among flat readings at levels 3^m, exactly one level both refines the τ shadow and keeps the frame's antimatter completion closed on the observed tower — **k=9**. One rung down (k=3) the generations collapse into the shadow; one rung up (k=27) the completion breaks — the conjugates of six of the nine occupied slots land on empty addresses, so at that reading antimatter would exist only for the first generation. The k=27 rung is thereby closed as a reading level (its capacity is real — 9 classes per τ — but its occupancy and closure fail), and hypothesis H1 of v15.461 is upgraded in kind: its *level* is no longer a stipulation but a forced consequence of closure + refinement given the observed tower; its remaining content is *flatness* — that the distinguished mode carries a flat reading at all (v15.467: a genuine flat 9-list, not assemblable by tripling). ## Chain of results (`k27_fold_selector.py`, exhaustive enumeration)

1. **The ladder test.** For level L = 3^m (m = 1, 2, 3, 4), let S_m = {(τ + 3g) mod L : τ ∈ {−1, 0, 1}, g ∈ {0, 1, 2}} be the observed tower's class set, capacity per τ = 3^{m−1} (the classes over one τ shadow as g ranges freely), and closure the predicate ∀c ∈ S_m: −c mod L ∈ S_m (the address form of the frame's antimatter completion, v15.388). Results: m=1 — capacity 1, occupied 3 of 3, closed, does not refine (the tower collapses to the τ shadow; no generation structure is visible). m=2 — capacity 3, occupied **9 of 9**, closed, refines: closure holds *by exhaustion*, the nine slots being the nine classes. m=3 — capacity 9, occupied 9 of 27, **not closed**, refines. m=4 — capacity 27, occupied 9 of 81, **not closed**, refines. Hence m=2 is the unique level satisfying both predicates.
2. **The k=27 failure in detail.** Of the nine occupied classes at level 27, only three have their conjugates occupied: the g=0 pair (τ=−1, g=0) ↔ (τ=+1, g=0) (classes 26 ↔ 1) and the origin (class 0, self-conjugate). Every g > 0 slot's conjugate lands on an empty class (e.g. (τ=0, g=1) at class 3 → conjugate 24; (τ=+1, g=2) at 7 → 20). Read literally, a 27-addressed world with the observed occupancy would have antimatter partners for the first generation only — contradicting the frame's completion, which is part of the frame's definition, not an added assumption. The would-be Z54 address (2 × 27) inherits the failure from its 27 component; the standing address remains Z18 (v15.467 §2).
3. **What is explained and what is input (stated plainly).** Inputs: the observed tower — three τ values with three generations each, antimatter-complete (all empirical facts of the SM table the frame was built to hold) — and flatness (H1's residue). Output: the level. The explanation's shape is exhaustion: an antimatter-complete tower whose slots exactly exhaust its reading level's classes has #slots = level; nine observed slots admit exactly one refining flat level, 9, and at that level the fold's fiber (3 per τ) is the generation count (v15.461). This does not derive the number 3 from the machinery alone — a hypothetical 4-generation tower (12 slots) would exhaust no 3^m level (12 is not a power of 3) and is exactly what the fold already excludes at level 9 by class duplication, v15.461: g and g+3 share an address, so two distinct generations would be indistinguishable to every class function on the addresses). What the selector removes is the *arbitrariness of the level*: previously "read at 9" was H1's free choice; it is now the only choice compatible with the frame's own completion.
4. **The digit rule one level deeper (for the record).** Negation on canonical digits (τ₀, d₁, d₂) of a class mod 27 is the mixed-radix borrow rule — τ₀' = (−τ₀) mod 3 with borrow b₁ = [τ₀ ≠ 0], d₁' = (−d₁ − b₁) mod 3 with borrow b₂ = [d₁ + b₁ ≠ 0], d₂' = (−d₂ − b₂) mod 3 — verified on all 27 classes. This is v15.461's rule (digit → −digit − [τ>0]) iterated: each digit's flip borrows from the digit below. It describes the (empty-address) conjugation at 27 exactly; its consistency does not rescue the level, since the images fall outside the occupied set.

## Negatives / scope limits, stated plainly

The selector operates **within flat readings**: it assumes the distinguished mode's grading is read modulo a flat 3^m (the fold's setting), and selects m. It does not prove flatness — the nested (Z3)^r readings of v15.467 are outside its scope (they carry no order-9 class and no fiber, so within them the fold does not exist to be leveled). Nor does it touch H2 (whether any sector reads the digit — v15.468, narrowed at v15.472 to the charged-lepton mass sector) or the frame↔canonical dictionary absence. The closure predicate uses the frame's antimatter completion as an internal consistency requirement; a hypothetical frame without that completion would not be subject to the selector, and the observed world's antimatter completeness is an empirical input, listed as such in §3. Level m=0 (no reading) trivially satisfies closure and is excluded by the refinement requirement, as is m=1.
