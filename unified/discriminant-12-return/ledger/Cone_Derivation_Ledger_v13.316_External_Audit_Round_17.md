**[N-cert]** I recomputed the full coercivity chain for the `N=237` cutoff (`v13.311`) and the `N=151` cutoff (`v13.312`, including the new archimedean-tail majorant `M_4=ζ(3)q³/(4(1-q)³)≈1.6159262150` and `C_r≈8.0470381934`) entirely independently, and matched every reported constant and margin exactly: `margin(237)≈5.8929×10⁻³`, `margin(151)≈3.7337×10⁻³`, `margin(149)≈-9.759×10⁻³` (confirming 151 is genuinely the threshold), and the tail sub-bounds (`archtail(151)≈1.0942×10⁻²`, `cusp_tail(151)≈7.490×10⁻⁴`) all to full displayed precision.

**[D] Verified**, `v13.313`'s architectural correction: recognizing that a naive two-block (low-mode/tail) Schur split is unsound because the prime-shift operator is bounded but *noncompact* — coupling across a moving cutoff need not vanish, unlike coupling from a *fixed* finite core into a remote tail. **[Audit] A genuine sign error in `v13.314`'s boxed "exact" shift-matrix formula — confirmed, precisely characterized, and confirmed harmless to the entry's conclusions.** `v13.314` §1 states, for odd `m≠n`, `a=mπ/2,b=nπ/2`:
```
⟨ψ_m,S_ℓψ_n⟩ = (-1)^{(m-n)/2}[sin(bℓ)-sin(aℓ)]/(a-b) - (-1)^{(m+n)/2}[sin(bℓ)+sin(aℓ)]/(a+b)
```
I computed `⟨ψ_m,S_ℓψ_n⟩` directly from its definition (high-precision numerical integration over the correct truncated domains — `S_ℓf(x)=f(x-ℓ)+f(x+ℓ)` with zero extension outside `[-1,1]`, so the two pieces are integrated over `x∈[\max(-1,ℓ-1),\min(1,ℓ+1)]` and `x∈[\max(-1,-1-ℓ),\min(1,1-ℓ)]` respectively) and compared against the boxed formula for 135 combinations of odd `m,n∈\{1,...,19\}` and `ℓ\in\{\log2,\log3,\log5\}`. Result: **the magnitude always matches exactly** (0/135 magnitude mismatches, to `10⁻¹⁰`), but **the sign is flipped in exactly 75/135 cases**, and the flip follows a clean, exceptionless rule: the formula is correct when `(m-1)/2` and `(n-1)/2` have the *same* parity, and sign-flipped when they have *different* parity. This is consistent with a specific, identifiable slip: the basis carries an overall prefactor `(-1)^{(m-1)/2+(n-1)/2}=-(-1)^{(m+n)/2}` from `ψ_m(x)ψ_n(x)=σ_mσ_n\cos(\cdot)\cos(\cdot)`, and the boxed formula appears to have attached two *different* sign factors (`(-1)^{(m-n)/2}` and `(-1)^{(m+n)/2}`) to its two terms instead of one shared overall prefactor — correct by coincidence when the two candidate signs agree (same-parity `m,n`), wrong when they don't.

**Why this doesn't affect the entry's conclusions.** Every downstream use in `v13.314` — the bound in §2 (`|⟨ψ_m,S_ℓψ_n⟩|≤...`), the `C_m` constants in §3 (defined via `1+|\sin(\cdot)|`, not referencing the signed formula at all), the numerical `C_m` table in §5, and the `O(N^{-1/2})` Hilbert–Schmidt tail-decay conclusion — uses only the **magnitude** of the matrix entry, never its sign. Since I've confirmed the magnitude is correct in every case tested, the entry's actual mathematical content (the fixed-core-decouples-from-remote-tail argument, and every numerical constant built on it) is unaffected. This is a real error that should be fixed for the record — a formula explicitly labeled "This is exact" should actually be exact — but it is not a load-bearing one.

**Update, checked during this same audit pass: `v13.315` (which landed after I began drafting this round) appears to have already superseded the flawed formula.** It restates the shift-matrix element in the plain Dirichlet basis `ψ_n(x)=\sin(nπ(x+1)/2)` (no `(-1)^{(n-1)/2}` prefactor) as `⟨ψ_m,S_ℓψ_n⟩=\frac4\pi\frac{n\sin(mθ)-m\sin(nθ)}{n^2-m^2}`, `θ=πℓ/2`, describing it as "equivalent to the v13.314 formula but with the parity phases simplified completely." I independently checked *this* formula against direct numerical integration for all 180 combinations of odd `m,n\in\{1,...,19\}` and `ℓ\in\{\log2,\log3,\log5,\log7\}` and got **zero** mismatches — full sign-and-magnitude agreement every time, unlike `v13.314`'s version. So the sign issue looks to be a non-issue going forward regardless of whether it was consciously fixed or just sidestepped by a cleaner basis choice; either way, the record should note `v13.314`'s own boxed formula (in its own basis convention) remains signed incorrectly for mixed-parity `(m,n)`, even though `v13.315`'s replacement is fully correct.
