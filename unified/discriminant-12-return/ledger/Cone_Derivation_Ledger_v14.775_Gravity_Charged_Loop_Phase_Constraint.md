For a circular loop of physical radius R, fixed tangent mechanical momentum p and magnetic flux Φ through the loop, the total phase with a chosen orientation convention is
\[
\Theta=(2\pi pR+q\Phi)/\hbar.
\]
Under single-valued U(1) gauge changes the holonomy exp(iΘ) is unchanged. Requiring exp(iΘ)=1 gives
\[
\boxed{pR+q\Phi/(2\pi)=m\hbar}.
\]
This refines the ungauged loop condition v14.774, with p or flux needed to set an absolute radius. The condition is not imposed in every quantum system; dynamical and geometric phases may modify the model. A gauge phase does not force h_G=1/2 or Newton's G.
