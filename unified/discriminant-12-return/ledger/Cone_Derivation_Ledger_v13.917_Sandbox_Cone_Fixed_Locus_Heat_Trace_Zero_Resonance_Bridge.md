## 1. Compression shells generate the full multiplicative state space [D]

Let the integer cone shell labeled by n have norm/scale coordinate

\[
r_n=\log n.
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm shell}=\ell^2(\mathbb N),
\qquad
R\,|n\rangle=(\log n)|n\rangle .
\]

Binary full compression identifies exactly the prime labels:

\[
b(n)=0
\iff
n\text{ is prime}
\]

apart from the separately treated vacuum label n=1.

By unique factorization,

\[
n=\prod_p p^{\nu_p(n)},
\]

so the shell basis has the exact occupation-number factorization

\[
|n\rangle
\longleftrightarrow
\bigotimes_p |\nu_p(n)\rangle ,
\]

i.e. the positive integers are the free commutative monoid generated by the prime/full-compression shells. In this representation,

\[
\boxed{
R=\sum_p (\log p)\,N_p
}
\]

on the natural finite-occupation domain, where \(N_p|\nu\rangle=\nu|\nu\rangle\).

For \(\Re s>1\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
Z_{\rm comp}(s)
&:=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_{\rm shell}}e^{-sR}\\
&=\sum_{n\ge1}e^{-s\log n}\\
&=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}\\
&=\prod_p\left(\sum_{k\ge0}p^{-ks}\right)\\
&=\boxed{\zeta(s)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the compression data do more than reveal prime locations: once unique factorization is applied, the primitive full-compression shells generate the entire Euler partition function.

No explicit formula is used in this construction.

### Logarithmic current [D]

Taking the logarithmic derivative,

\[
-\partial_s\log Z_{\rm comp}(s)
=
\sum_{p,k\ge1}(\log p)p^{-ks}
=
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^s}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
-\partial_s\log Z_{\rm comp}(s)
=
-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s)
}
\]

for \(\Re s>1\).

This recovers exactly the prime-power current of v13.767 and identifies v13.895's
\(\Lambda\)-jump / log-lcm current as the thermodynamic logarithmic scale current of the shell partition.

## 3. The factor-exchange fixed arithmetic locus is the square lattice [D]

From v13.573/574, on the fixed-n factor hyperbola

\[
T^2-X^2=n,
\qquad
Y^2=n,
\]

factor exchange is \(F:X\mapsto-X\). Its arithmetic fixed-point condition is

\[
X=0
\iff
n=m^2.
\]

Thus the integer factor-exchange fixed shells are exactly the squares.

On shell \(n=m^2\), the fixed incidence is

\[
(X,Y,T)=(0,\pm m,m).
\]

Adjoining the cone apex \(m=0\), the signed root-sheet coordinate supplies the integer fixed lattice

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_F\simeq\mathbb Z,
\qquad
Y=m.
}
\]

On this locus the cone invariant is

\[
Y^2=T^2=m^2.
\]

Define the fixed-locus quadratic Hamiltonian

\[
H_F|m\rangle=m^2|m\rangle .
\]

Its Gaussian heat trace is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_F(x)
=
\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\pi xH_F}
=
\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}e^{-\pi m^2x}
=
\vartheta(x).
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\psi(x)
=
\sum_{m\ge1}e^{-\pi m^2x}
=
\frac12\bigl(\Theta_F(x)-1\bigr).
\]

Thus the classical Jacobi theta series used in v13.717 is exactly the heat trace of the cone's arithmetic factor-exchange fixed lattice.

**Guardrail.** The Gaussian heat semigroup is standard harmonic-analysis structure on the quadratic lattice \(Y^2=m^2\). The statement above is exact once that canonical heat kernel is chosen; this entry does not claim that the bare incidence geometry alone uniquely forces the heat-semigroup formalism.

## 7. Full complex zero condition and the critical-line simplification [D/I]

Let

\[
\rho=\beta+i\gamma,
\qquad
w=\rho-\frac12=\alpha+i\gamma,
\qquad
\alpha=\beta-\frac12.
\]

Since \(\Phi\) is real and even,

\[
\Xi(\alpha+i\gamma)
=
2\int_0^\infty
\Phi(\tau)\cosh\bigl((\alpha+i\gamma)\tau\bigr)\,d\tau.
\]

Using

\[
\cosh((\alpha+i\gamma)\tau)
=
\cosh(\alpha\tau)\cos(\gamma\tau)
+
i\sinh(\alpha\tau)\sin(\gamma\tau),
\]

the exact zero condition is the pair

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty
\Phi(\tau)\cosh(\alpha\tau)\cos(\gamma\tau)\,d\tau=0,
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty
\Phi(\tau)\sinh(\alpha\tau)\sin(\gamma\tau)\,d\tau=0.
}
\]

On the critical line \(\alpha=0\), the second condition vanishes identically by symmetry, leaving

\[
\boxed{
\Xi(i\gamma)
=
2\int_0^\infty\Phi(\tau)\cos(\gamma\tau)\,d\tau
=
0.
}
\]

Thus every critical-line zero ordinate is exactly a cosine-cancellation frequency of the cone fixed-locus/Casimir heat response.

**Interpretation [I].** \(\alpha=0\) is the balanced two-sided heat-scale slice; \(\alpha\neq0\) exponentially tilts the two orientations. RH would say that every nontrivial zero resonance occurs on the balanced slice. This is only a geometric reformulation, not an RH proof. Positivity of \(\Phi\) does not rule out off-axis complex zeros.
