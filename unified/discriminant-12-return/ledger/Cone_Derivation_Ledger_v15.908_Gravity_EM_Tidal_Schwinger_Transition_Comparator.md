**[D] Magnetic comparator:** For two physical isotropic planar oscillator coordinates q1,q2 with mass M, frequency omega*, and charged particle q in weak perpendicular uniform B, the linear minimal-coupling term is H_B=−qB L_z/(2M), with L_z=2 hbar J_y. Consequently H_B=−hbar(qB/M)J_y. The strength qB/M is a physical input; the diamagnetic B² term is not included in this first-order comparator. Physical oscillator coordinates are not the Cone's X,Y without an independent map.

**[D/C] Tidal comparator:** A local gravitational potential-per-mass Hessian off-diagonal entry Phi12 generates V_cross=M Phi12 q1 q2. With q_i=sqrt[hbar/(2M omega*)](a_i+a_i†), the cross-term is [hbar Phi12/(2omega*)](ab+ab†+a†b+a†b†). Its number-conserving rotating-wave approximation is H_tidal,RWA=hbar(Phi12/omega*)J_x. The full interaction includes pair creation/annihilation across N shells; using RWA requires weak coupling and fast detuning of those terms. Phi12 is zero in principal axes of a diagonal static source; an appropriately oriented physical detector or nontrivial offdiagonal tidal field is required. These are standard matter interactions, not newly derived gravitational physics from Cone.

**[D] Exact fixed-shell amplitudes:** Pair (n,k) maps to |n−1,k>. Both J_x and J_y give right-neighbor magnitude sqrt(nk)/2, left-neighbor magnitude sqrt[(n−1)(k+1)]/2. The phase of J_y relative to J_x differs, but a single isolated phase can be removed by a J_z basis rotation; a physical phase comparison requires an independent reference and a gauge-invariant experiment.
