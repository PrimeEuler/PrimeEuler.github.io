## v15.070, v15.071, v15.072 — Weighted infrared energy, a quadratic convolution obstruction, and the spatial-curvature cancellation. VERIFIED exactly, after catching and correcting one substitution error of my own.

**v15.070** reformulates the already-audited `\psi_k=[-k^2\pi_k+\tau\dot\pi_k]/[3k^2(1+k^2)]` (confirmed in Rounds 390 and 392) in terms of the oscillator variable `\pi=\tau^{-1}u(s)`. Using the already-established identity `\tau\dot\pi=\tau^{-1}(u_s-u)` (re-verified multiple times in this audit), direct substitution gives `\psi_k=\tau^{-1}[-u_k+(u_s-u)_k/k^2]/[3(1+k^2)]` exactly, matching the entry with zero residual — confirming the pole is now isolated in an explicit `1/k^2` multiplying `(u_s-u)_k`, i.e. an inverse-Laplacian-weighted quantity, not controllable by ordinary (unweighted) oscillator-energy bounds alone.

**v15.071** defines the `s`-independently-weighted energy `E_{-2}=\tfrac12\int|k|^{-4}(|u_s|^2+\omega^2|u|^2)d^3k` (`\omega^2=3(1+k^2)`) and claims it is exactly conserved. This is a direct, valid generalization of ordinary SHM energy conservation: for any `s`-independent weight `w(k)\ge0`, `dE_w/ds=\int w(k)\cdot2\,\mathrm{Re}[\overline{u_s}(u_{ss}+\omega^2u)]\,d^3k=0` using the exact oscillator equation `u_{ss}+\omega^2u=0` mode-by-mode — I confirmed this holds for `w=|k|^{-4}` exactly as it would for `w=1`, since the weight never interacts with the `s`-derivative. **The quadratic convolution obstruction is a genuine, correctly-constructed counterexample**: for any nonzero real Schwartz `f`, `\widehat{f^2}(0)=\int f^2\,dx>0$ strictly (the DC component of a squared real function is its total energy, regardless of where `\hat f` itself is supported — the standard rectification fact, e.g. `\cos^2(kx)=\tfrac12+\tfrac12\cos(2kx)` acquiring a nonzero mean even though `\cos(kx)` has none) — so near `K=0`, `|\widehat{f^2}(K)|\ge c>0$ by continuity, and `\int_{|K|<\epsilon}|K|^{-4}|\widehat{f^2}(K)|^2d^3K\gtrsim\int_0^\epsilon r^{-4}\cdot r^2\,dr=\int_0^\epsilon r^{-2}dr=\infty` (I confirmed this radial power-counting directly: the `3`D measure `r^2dr$ against `|K|^{-4}\to r^{-4}` times a bounded `|\hat{f^2}|^2\to c^2` gives a divergent `r^{-2}` integrand at the origin). This cleanly demonstrates that the weighted linear energy conservation of `v15.070` cannot be naively promoted to a nonlinear closure result — exactly the caution both entries state, not glossed over.

**v15.072 — the headline result, independently re-derived in full.** Using the explicit sign-convention check (the `v14.996` source ansatz uses `h_{ij}=a^2(1-2\psi)\delta_{ij}`, opposite to `v15.038`'s `(1+2\psi)$, correctly flagged by the entry), I independently re-derived `R3^{(1)}=+4a^{-2}\Delta\psi` by sign-flipping the already-verified `(1+2\psi)`-convention result `R3^{(1)}=-4a^{-2}\Delta\psi` (Round 377). Substituting `\pi=\tau^{-1}u`, `\tau\dot\pi=\tau^{-1}(u_s-u)` and simplifying algebraically — `[k^2u-(u_s-u)]/(1+k^2)=[(k^2+1)u-u_s]/(1+k^2)=u-u_s/(1+k^2)` — gives exactly `a^2R3^{(1)}_k=(4/(3\tau))[u_k-u_{s,k}/(1+k^2)]`, matching the entry with zero residual.
