## 9. [D][G] Ladder radius and Casimir/precession radius

## 10. [D][G] Ordinary versus twisted sheet crossing

Ordinary sheet reversal is

\[
C_T:(X,Y,T)\mapsto(X,Y,-T).
\]

For

\[
L_{XY}=X\partial_Y-Y\partial_X,
\]

\[
\boxed{C_TL_{XY}C_T^{-1}=L_{XY}}.
\]

The foliation exchange is

\[
J_{\rm fol}:(X,Y,T)\mapsto(Y,X,T).
\]

For the twisted crossing

\[
G=J_{\rm fol}C_T,
\]

\[
\boxed{GL_{XY}G^{-1}=-L_{XY}}.
\]

Thus ordinary sheet crossing preserves precession orientation, while twisted crossing reverses it.
