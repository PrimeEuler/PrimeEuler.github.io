## 1. Exact coefficient identities

For `a=1`, use the normalized direct Dirichlet basis

\[
\psi_n(x)=\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi(x+1)}2\right),
\qquad
k_n=\frac{n\pi}{2}.
\]

If

\[
v_M=\sum_{n=1}^{M}c_n^{(M)}\psi_n,
\]

then orthonormality gives exactly

\[
\|v_M\|_{L^2}^2=\sum_n |c_n^{(M)}|^2,
\]

and differentiation gives

\[
\|Dv_M\|_{L^2}^2=\sum_n k_n^2|c_n^{(M)}|^2.
\]

For a smaller vector embedded into a larger Ritz space, with sign chosen to maximize overlap,

\[
\|v_N-v_M\|_{L^2}^2=\sum_n |\Delta c_n|^2,
\]

\[
\|D(v_N-v_M)\|_{L^2}^2=\sum_n k_n^2|\Delta c_n|^2.
\]

Because `u_M=Dv_M`, strong convergence of `v_M` in `H_0^1` is equivalent to strong `L^2` convergence of `u_M`.

Therefore the available sequence does **not** yet justify the statement that the Ritz candidates are numerically Cauchy in `H_0^1`.

## 4. What is established numerically

The combined v13.291–v13.294 evidence now supports the following numerical classification:

- finite Ritz minima remain positive at arbitrary precision through the tested sections while collapsing rapidly toward zero;
- the corresponding lowest vectors are strongly aligned in `L^2` and remain concentrated at low frequency;
- boundary mass decreases rather than increases;
- independently evaluated `P_1G_1u_M` residuals decrease strongly through M=12;
- the projected pole/prime/archimedean action vectors cancel mode-by-mode to very high precision inside the Ritz space;
- derivative norms remain bounded through M=14.

These facts make simple spectral pollution by high-frequency or boundary escape increasingly implausible.

## 5. What remains open

The present computation does **not** establish any of the following:

1. that the full sequence `v_M` converges in `H_0^1`;
2. that `u_M=Dv_M` converges strongly in `L^2`;
3. that there exists a nonzero limit `u` satisfying `G_1u=0` or `P_1G_1u=0` in the infinite-dimensional problem;
4. that `lambda_1=0`;
5. RH or GRH.

A bounded sequence in `H_0^1(-1,1)` has weakly convergent subsequences and is relatively compact in `L^2`, but converting the present finite-dimensional numerical evidence into a theorem requires certified bounds and a rigorous passage to the operator limit.  No such theorem is claimed here.

## 6. Next target

The next audit should resolve whether the non-monotone successive differences are a mode-count artifact or genuine drift.  The most useful continuation is to extend the high-precision even-v sector to M=16,18,20 if numerically feasible, while reusing the one-dimensional component matrices and exploiting parity from the outset.  This should report:

- `||Dv_M||_2`;

- odd-index coefficient stabilization;
- off-grid `P_1G_1u_M` residuals;

If the derivative differences then decay consistently while the operator residual also decays, the numerical case for a genuine limiting kernel candidate would become substantially stronger.  If derivative differences plateau or grow, the current kernel interpretation would need to be weakened.
