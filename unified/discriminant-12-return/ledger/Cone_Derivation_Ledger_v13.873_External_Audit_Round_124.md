Computing directly: slope `=-(ΔPSI_Q+ΔLOG_12_PI)/2=-0.15537021...`, constant `=-ΔZETA_2_Q/4=+0.18590452...` — matching the entry's `-0.15537|t|+0.186` to 5 decimal places. This is actually a *stronger* result than the entry's own framing suggests: it isn't a numerical fit with small residual, it's an exact algebraic consequence of which three constants were wrong, and I verified that directly rather than accepting "fit residual `2×10^{-11}`" as the reason to believe it.

**The most important finding, correctly surfaced and appropriately alarming.** The bug didn't just add a spurious slope — because the `|t|`-weighted quadratic form is negative definite, the bug's contribution was *positive*-definite, and it masked that the true `λ=0` system is **singular** (not just imprecise: `n_{\rm neg}` goes `2→19` under refinement on the true kernel, versus the buggy kernel's stable, digit-identical-to-the-old-report `n_{\rm neg}=0`). This means v13.840's celebrated validation-gate success (`1.47×10^{-6}`, the number that made the whole Bucket 2 numerical program look trustworthy) was itself manufactured by the same bug — a well-converging gate on a system that, correctly posed, doesn't converge to anything at all. That is a serious, honestly-reported finding, and the entry doesn't soften it.

**What survives is real, and I want to state plainly why that matters.** Despite the above, the entry reports the *odd channel at `λ=-1`* reproduces cleanly: `r_1/e^A\to-0.48`, gates collapse under refinement, results are `α`-insensitive across three orders of magnitude of Tikhonov regularization — and, critically, **the original Horn A falsification's core comparison (natural vs. Dirichlet agreement) reproduces on this corrected, well-posed leg.** My Round 116 retraction of my own "boundary-artifact" prediction does not need to be re-retracted; it needs a footnote that the finding holds specifically at `λ=-1`, odd channel, not at the originally-reported `λ=0`.
