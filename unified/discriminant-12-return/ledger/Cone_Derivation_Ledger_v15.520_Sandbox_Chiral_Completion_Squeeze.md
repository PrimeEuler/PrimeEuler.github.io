2. **Jarlskog witnesses (seeded numerics, 300 random unitaries per case).** At N = 2, max |J| = 1.25×10⁻¹⁶ over the samples — J vanishes identically, so a chiral two-generation theory cannot violate CP through mixing at *any* parameter values. At N = 3, J is generically nonzero (max 0.0898; 90% of samples with |J| > 0.005). Mirror check: J(conj V) = −J(V) exactly — the supplied bit's *value* flips CP's sign, not its existence; the floor depends on chirality, not on which side nature picked.
