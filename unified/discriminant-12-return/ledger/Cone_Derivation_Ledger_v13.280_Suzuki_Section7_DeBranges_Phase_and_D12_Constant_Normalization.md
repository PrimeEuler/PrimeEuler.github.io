## 1. Source-established Section 7 de Branges structure

He introduces the infinite Weil energy space

\[
\mathcal H(A_\infty)
\]

and, using his earlier de Branges work, an isometric isomorphism

\[
\boxed{U:\mathcal H(A_\infty)\to\mathcal B}

onto a de Branges space `B`.

Let `M` be multiplication by the spectral variable on `B`. Suzuki states that `M` has deficiency indices `(1,1)` and that the self-adjoint extension

\[
\boxed{M_{\pi/2}}

Writing

\[
\mathsf D_{\pi/2}=U^{-1}M_{\pi/2}U,

Thus the source-established infinite extension parameter in the de Branges parameterization is `psi=pi/2`, while the boundary vector parameter used in Section 7.8 below is `theta=pi`. These are two different parameter conventions and must not be identified numerically without tracking the formulas.

## 2. The canonical Hermite–Biehler function

Suzuki's de Branges space is generated by

\[
\boxed{
E(z)=\xi(1/2-iz)+\xi'(1/2-iz).
}
\]

Its reproducing kernel is

\[
K(w,z)
=
\frac{E(z)\overline{E(w)}-E^\sharp(z)\overline{E^\sharp(w)}}
{2\pi i(\bar w-z)}.
\]

The vectors

\[
K(-i,\cdot),\qquad K(i,\cdot)
\]

span the two deficiency spaces of `M^*`, and Suzuki computes that they have equal norm.

He then defines the boundary vector

\[
W_\theta(t)=K(-i,t)+e^{i\theta}K(i,t).
\]

For the de Branges boundary form, the exact source identity is

\[
\boxed{
W(K(\bar z,\cdot),W_\theta)
=
-\frac{e^{-i\theta/2}}{\pi i}
\left(C_\theta E(z)-\overline{C_\theta}E^\sharp(z)\right),
}
\]

where

\[
C_\theta
=
\xi(3/2)\cos(\theta/2)
+i\xi'(3/2)\sin(\theta/2).
\]

This formula gives the exact infinite boundary-phase dependence.

## 4. Exact source derivation of the Corollary-1.6 target

Suzuki introduces functions `f_z` through

\[
\widehat{f_z}(t)=\frac{K(\bar z,t)}{E(t)}.
\]

His Section 7.8 computation gives, for general `theta`,

\[
(z-i)\widehat{f_{+i}}(z)
+e^{i\theta}(z+i)\widehat{f_{-i}}(z)
=
\frac{i e^{i\theta/2}}{\pi^2}
\left(
C_\theta-\overline{C_\theta}\frac{E^\sharp(z)}{E(z)}
\right).

At `theta=pi` this becomes

\[
\boxed{
(z-i)\widehat{f_{+i}}(z)
-(z+i)\widehat{f_{-i}}(z)
=
\frac{2\xi'(3/2)}{\pi^2 i}
\frac{\xi(1/2-iz)}{E(z)}.

Since

\[
E(z)=\xi(1/2-iz)+\xi'(1/2-iz),

the exact infinite characteristic ratio is

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\xi(1/2-iz)}
{\xi(1/2-iz)+\xi'(1/2-iz)}.

\]

This source identity directly produces Corollary 1.6.

It also shows that the natural infinite normalization is only the constant

\[
\boxed{
\frac{2\xi'(3/2)}{\pi^2 i},

once the canonical deficiency vectors are used.

Therefore Suzuki's arbitrary-looking exponential factor `exp(phi(a,z))` is best interpreted as allowing finite-volume normalization drift; the Section-7 model itself does not require a nonconstant entire correction.
