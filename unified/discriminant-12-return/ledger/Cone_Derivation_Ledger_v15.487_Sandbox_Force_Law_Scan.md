Two bodies therefore determine exactly the invariants (m₁, m₂, p₁·p₂ ~ m₁m₂ cosh Δψ) — 1+1-dimensional momentum data, with no positional content. Exhibit: two configurations with identical addresses (m₁ = 1, ψ₁ = 0.2; m₂ = 2, ψ₂ = −0.1) at separations r = 1 and r = 4 have Newtonian accelerations 2.0000 and 0.1250 (ratio 16) while *every* function of the addresses takes the same value on both. A force law distinguishes them; no address function can. This is not a search failure over a candidate family — it is a content statement about what the addresses are. (Position enters the dynamics chain only through the second, crossing parabola pair — which is why Level 2 is a separate, granted premise.)
