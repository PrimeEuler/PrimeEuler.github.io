   labels — gauge-invariant, not gauge choices. The mass shell

   the orbit, J labels the little-group representation carried on
   it. This is the same grammar as the trajectory cones on record
   (v15.229, v15.230): the pair charge s selects the cone at fixed
   |J| = j + ½. A scalar label picks the surface; spin lives on it.
2. **Where the gauge is.** (i) The rest point on the orbit is a
   frame choice. (ii) Spin transport along the orbit: boosts do
   not commute, so a transported spin acquires a Wigner rotation;
   continuously, Thomas precession — a connection on the mass
   shell with measurable holonomy. (iii) In the charge sectors,
   Dirac-string (EM) / Misner-string (TN) placement is the gauge
   freedom, with eg = s the invariant label.

   j through |κ| = j + ½ (computed: 2P3/2 − 2P1/2 = 4.528e-5 eV

   inside that number: naive rest-frame coefficient g/2 = 1.00116

   correction the spin-orbit splitting is twice too large, so the
   fine structure reads out the transport holonomy. On the
   parabolic side, hydrogen's states |n₁n₂m⟩ are the mesh
   crossings (Paper C) and the mesh offset k = n₁ − n₂ = 2X is
   the Stark quantum number, ΔE = (3/2)nkF (n = 2: ±3F, 0;
