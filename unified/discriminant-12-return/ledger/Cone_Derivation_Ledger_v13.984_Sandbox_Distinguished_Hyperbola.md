## 1. Experiment [N]

Different planar conic sections through the cone, R at zeta zeros

| section | observable | R |

## 2. Result [N]

Only the **xy=n binary** (the fold decision d=2 vs d>2 at √n)
carries the strong spectrum (R=2.17). The raw lattice count
d(n) is weak (R=1.16). The circle is suppressed at zeta (R=0.73)
with only a faint χ₄ whisper (R=1.33). The x²−y² hyperbola
is null (R=0.97).

## 3. Interpretation [I]

The xy=n hyperbola is **distinguished**: it is the multiplication
table itself, and the encoding runs through the *binary fold
decision* at √n, not the raw count. Bounded sections (circle)
cannot fully encode; only the unbounded hyperbola leaving the
cone's boundary does. The V₄/mod-12 structure is forced by the
{2,3}-sieve dominance (2·3=6, primes >3 are 6±1, mod 12 gives
four classes), not imposed.
