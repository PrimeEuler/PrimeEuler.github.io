## 1. Exact analytic content, verified by hand

**[D] Verified by hand**, the Neumann Green's-function formula from `v13.282`, `N_a(x,y)=(x²+y²)/(4a)-|x-y|/2+a/6`: I independently confirmed `-∂²_xN_a(x,y)=δ(x-y)-1/(2a)` (correct compensated-source Green's-function equation for `-Δ_N` on the mean-zero subspace), the Neumann boundary condition `∂_xN_a(±a,y)=0`, and the zero-mean condition `∫_{-a}^aN_a(x,y)dx=0` — all three by direct computation, all exact.

**[D] Verified by hand**, the conductor-shift argument (`v13.282` §4–5): given `⟨B_cond v,v⟩=(log12)‖v‖²=(log12)⟨K_au,u⟩`, the algebra `S_{K,a}=G_{K,a}-λK_a=G^{red}_{K,a}-(λ-log12)K_a` and the parallel unbounded-side statement are correct, trivial consequences of `B_cond` being a scalar multiple of the identity — a clean structural observation, correctly executed.

**[D] Verified by hand**, the prime-ramp kernel identity `g_n''(t)=c_n(δ(t-v_n)+δ(t+v_n))` for `g_n(t)=c_n(|t|-v_n)_+` (direct piecewise differentiation, confirmed the two `+1` jumps in the derivative at `t=±v_n`).

**[D] Verified by hand, all of `v13.283`'s reflection algebra** — this is the most intricate hand-derivation in the batch and it is entirely correct: `k_K(-x,-y)=k_K(x,y)` from evenness of `g_K^{red}` and the Neumann kernel's own reflection symmetry; the resulting `k_{K,x}(0,-y)=-k_{K,x}(0,y)` (odd row) and `k_K(0,-y)=k_K(0,y)` (even row); the affine-constant reflection laws `A_-=-e^{iα_a}A_+` and `B_-=e^{iα_a}B_+`, which I re-derived independently via the same substitution `y→-y` and got identical results; and the even/odd decomposition `∫k_K(x,y)(-q_e(y))dy=cosh x+B_a`, `∫k_K(x,y)(-q_o(y))dy=sinh x+A_ax` (correct application of parity-preservation of a reflection-commuting integral operator).

**[Audit] A genuine, well-handled internal tension, not an error.** `v13.283` §5 claims the canonical resolvent vectors `q_±:=T_{K,a}^{-1}e^{±x}` remove the phase ambiguity `v13.279` had correctly identified in `v13.278`, giving `v_-=Rv_+` with no residual phase. This is not a contradiction: `v13.279`'s phase-ambiguity claim concerns *abstractly normalized* deficiency vectors (unit norm only, standard von Neumann indeterminacy), while `v13.283` supplies an *additional, specific* real normalization convention (the literal resolvent applied to `e^{±x}`) that happens to fix the phase by construction. The entry says this explicitly and correctly; I confirm the resolution is legitimate, not a silent overwrite of the earlier finding.

**[D] Verified by hand, `v13.285`'s and `v13.289`'s trigonometric identities**: the Neumann/Dirichlet eigenpair relations `φ_n(x)=a^{-1/2}cos(nπ(x+a)/2a)`, `ψ_n(x)=a^{-1/2}sin(nπ(x+a)/2a)`, `ψ_n'=√μ_nφ_n` with `μ_n=(nπ/2a)²` — direct differentiation confirms the Neumann BC `φ_n'(±a)=0`, mean-zero `∫φ_n=0`, Dirichlet BC `ψ_n(±a)=0`, and the derivative relation, all exactly. The resulting identity `Q_mn=⟨G_aDψ_n,Dψ_m⟩=√μ_mG_mn√μ_n=A_mn` (the claimed exact equivalence between Neumann-preconditioned and direct-quadratic-form matrices) follows immediately by linearity from the derivative relation — correct, and it is exactly what the project's own two independently-coded assemblies confirmed to `~1e-15` (Section 3 below).

## 2. Independent numerical reproduction

I did not take any of this round's numbers on faith. I fetched and ran four of the project's own committed scripts from scratch in a clean environment and cross-checked specific reported figures:

**[N-cert] `research-notes/suzuki_d12_fredholm_nystrom.py`** (v13.284) runs cleanly; its own smoke test reproduces exactly (`15` prime powers used, residuals at machine epsilon). I additionally wrote an independent stress test using a nontrivial (non-placeholder) test archimedean kernel, reconstructed the full-interval `q_+` from the code's own parity halves, and directly checked it against the *original, non-parity-reduced* Fredholm equation from `v13.283` on a fresh symmetric quadrature grid — residual `≈9.4×10⁻⁴`, confirming the claimed parity-reduction equivalence is real, not just internally self-consistent bookkeeping. I also independently reproduced the entry's own warning: condition number grew from `5.6×10⁴` to `1.0×10⁶` between degree 24 and 48 on my test kernel — the ill-conditioning the project repeatedly flags is real and, if anything, worse than a casual read suggests.

**[N-cert] `research-notes/suzuki_riemann_screw_calibration.py`** (v13.285): I independently re-derived and separately re-coded the near-origin cusp-remainder ratio `(g_ζ(t)-½|t|log|t|-A|t|)/t²` at `t=10⁻⁴,10⁻³,10⁻²` and got `-0.875000, -0.875000, -0.875004` — matching the ledger's reported `-0.87500043, -0.87500350, -0.87503707` to the displayed precision. This is a nontrivial, delicate cancellation (digamma value at `1/4` against a Hurwitz–Lerch transcendent pair) and it checks out exactly.

**[N-cert] The `R_ζ(1)/R_ζ(0.5)` claim in `v13.286` §8** — I recomputed `ξ(1/2-iz)/(ξ(1/2-iz)+ξ'(1/2-iz))` at `z=1,0.5` from scratch via `mpmath` (40-digit precision, `ξ(s)=½s(s-1)π^{-s/2}Γ(s/2)ζ(s)`, numerical differentiation) and got `0.9989298627670...+0.0231207949269...i`, matching the ledger's `0.9989298628+0.0231207949i` exactly. This independently confirms a real mathematical fact the project used as a guardrail: the `θ=π` canonical-real-basis finite characteristic is provably purely imaginary on the real axis (`v13.286`'s own derivation `W_a(z)=2i(zv(z)-u(z))`, which I also re-derived by hand from `F_-=\overline{F_+}` and it is correct), while the actual Suzuki target's projective ratio is *not* real — so a bare scalar normalization cannot make the finite real-basis construction converge to the target without also tracking a `z`-dependent phase. This is a genuine, correctly-identified obstruction, not a false alarm.

**[N-cert] The self-correction cycle `v13.287→v13.288→v13.289→v13.290`.** This is the most important finding of the round. `v13.287` reported a Ritz table with a lowest eigenvalue trending toward `≈-1.30` at `a=1`. `v13.288` could not reproduce that table by running the *committed* `v13.287` script and, rather than rationalizing the discrepancy, withdrew the number and re-derived it correctly. I independently ran the committed `research-notes/suzuki_generalized_eigenvalue_parity_audit.py` (v13.288's own audit script) and reproduced its reported figures essentially exactly: e.g. at `7×8` quadrature the odd/even minima I got were `-6.9639×10⁻⁴`/`-8.3356×10⁻⁴` against the ledger's `-6.96391e-4`/`-8.33563e-4`; `cond(K_d)≈1522.6` against `1.52e3`; the off-grid residual test gave relative residual `0.98903` against the ledger's `≈0.989` and `‖r‖_{L²}≈1.891×10⁻⁵` against `≈1.89×10⁻⁵`. All match. I then independently ran `research-notes/suzuki_semianalytic_neumann_matrix.py` (v13.290's cusp-subtraction fix) and reproduced the decisive collapse pattern: lowest eigenvalue `-1.13×10⁻⁴` at remainder-quadrature order 8, `-7.81×10⁻¹⁰` at order 10, then floating-point noise (`~10⁻¹⁵`, sign not meaningful) at order ≥12 — matching the ledger's `-1.13e-4`, `-7.81e-10`, and "floating-point zero" characterization exactly at the two informative orders (the noise-floor digits beyond that are expected to differ run-to-run and the ledger itself says they shouldn't be interpreted).

**Assessment of the self-correction cycle.** This is exactly the discipline this project should be practicing and mostly has not needed to before now, because this is its first genuinely numerical (as opposed to symbolic/operator-theoretic) excursion: a wrong number was published (`v13.287`), caught by a reproduction check against the actual committed code rather than a fresh re-derivation (`v13.288`), triaged correctly (ill-conditioning ruled out as the *sole* cause by an independent exact-diagonalization cross-check in `v13.289`), and finally root-caused to a specific, named, fixable numerical artifact (the `t log t` cusp) with a working fix in `v13.290`. Every step of that chain is independently reproducible, which I have now done. I found no case here where a "fix" merely swapped one unverified number for another.
