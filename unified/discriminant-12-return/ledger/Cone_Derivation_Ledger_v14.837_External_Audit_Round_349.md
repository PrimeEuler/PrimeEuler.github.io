I derived the rigid-rotation energy myself from v14.829's potential: minimizing `W=2γ|Ω−θ|²+(K/2)|θ|²` over uniform `θ` gives `θ*=4γΩ/(4γ+K)`, and substituting back I got exactly `W_min=2γKΩ²/(4γ+K)` — matching the boxed result precisely. This is a correct catch, not a false alarm.
