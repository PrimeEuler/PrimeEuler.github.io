## 1. What is and is not affected

The following already-audited results remain intact:

1. v14.034 / v14.036: the finite M=8000, mu=1 shifted front is positive outwardly;
2. v14.033 / v14.037: the retained four-channel far Schur contribution has a rigorous outward bound;
3. the rigorous shifted raw far floors and all scalar interval/source-faithful certificates remain unchanged.

The only issue is v14.027 section 6(c): the statement that the leftover front-to-far coupling after the four retained channels is uniformly negligible on all n>8000 by direct appeal to the v14.020 K=10 source-remainder estimate.

That adaptation is not valid for the full M=8000 front because the finite front contains modes m<=8000, so for the first far modes

\[
\sup_{m\le8000}\frac{m}{n}\to1.

v14.020 instead treated a source vector supported only through N=4000; its n>=8000 geometric ratio obeys m/n<=1/2.

## 2. Exact reason for the obstruction [D]

For the Cauchy/displacement kernel

\[
K(n,m)=\frac{2}{\pi}\frac{z_n m-nz_m}{n^2-m^2},
\]

the inverse-power expansion is generated by

\[
\frac1{1-r^2}
=\sum_{j=0}^{K}r^{2j}+\frac{r^{2K+2}}{1-r^2},
\qquad r=m/n.
\]

After the first four scalar channels (j=0,1 plus the first two pole terms) the omitted Cauchy part still contains

\[
\frac{r^4}{1-r^2}.
\]

Even after K=10, the geometric factor is

\[
\frac{r^{22}}{1-r^2}.
\]

For m=7999 and n=8001,

\[
r=\frac{7999}{8001}\approx0.99975003,
\]

so neither factor is small. Therefore no n>8000 uniform small-remainder theorem follows from the v14.020 separated-tail argument when the support extends to m=8000.

## 4. Recovery with separation [N/I]

The same adversarial replay shows rapid recovery once the remote row is separated from the entire M=8000 front.

At n approximately 16000, where m/n<=1/2:

### even

\[
\frac{\|R_{K=10}^{\rm Cauchy}\|_2}{\|B_{\rm exact}^{\rm Cauchy}\|_2}
\approx3.88\times10^{-8};
\]

### odd

\[
\frac{\|R_{K=10}^{\rm Cauchy}\|_2}{\|B_{\rm exact}^{\rm Cauchy}\|_2}
\approx4.46\times10^{-8}.
\]

At n approximately 24000 these fall to about 4.7e-12, and at 32000 to about 1e-14.

Therefore the high-order geometric method is sound; the error was only applying its separated-tail conclusion at the unseparated M=8000 boundary.

## 5. Corrected theorem architecture [D/I]

The v14.027 two-stage proof must replace its section 6(c) consumer by a near/far split:

\[
Q_{>8000}=Q_{\rm near}\oplus Q_{\rm sep},
\]

with, for example,

\[
Q_{\rm near}: 8000<n<16000,
\qquad
Q_{\rm sep}: n\ge16000.
\]

The requirements are:

1. preserve the exact/correlated front-to-near coupling on Q_near; do not absolute-bound it as a small geometric remainder;
2. merge that finite near block into a Schur/Feshbach calculation before any norm loss;
3. only on Q_sep, where m/n<=1/2 for every front mode m<=8000, apply the signed-moment hierarchy and geometric K=10 remainder;
4. retain the already-audited v14.033 four-channel theorem as a valid bound for the four-channel component, but do not infer a total far bound until the exact near remainder is included.

A second valid route is to eliminate the N=4000 retained block first and work directly with the remote Schur operator, so that the near shell is treated exactly before the separated far expansion.
