## 1. Two pins, two kinds of furniture
The cone is dimensionless until two pins are hung on it, and they
pin different things. **The null edge pins a ratio:** velocity is a
slope in the (X, T) plane and the edge is the unit slope
ΔX = ΔT — the locus where the space unit and the time unit are
declared equal. Every velocity is a fraction of the edge's slope;
acceleration, a rate of change of slope, inherits the pin. The SI
itself pins there: since 1983 the meter is defined via c (the
distance light travels in 1/299792458 s), so the meter is derived
from the second at the null slope. Edge slope = 1 is structural;
299792458 is the occupant hung on it.
**The circle pins a quantum:** ħ is the action per turn of the
phase circle (v14.620; phase = action/ħ, quantisation =
single-valuedness). c converts the cone's axes to each other
(kinematics); ħ converts winding counts to action (dynamics).
Neither pin does the other's job.

## 2. The treaty: E = ħω = ħkc = pc
The pins meet multiplicatively at energy. E = ħω reads: joules =
(joule-seconds per turn) × (turns per second). The per-second of ω
and the per-meter of k are counts on the edge's two axes, and
because the edge locks the axes at slope 1, a rider of the edge has
one count, not two — hence E = ħkc = pc. Three exact identities
mark the interplay: (i) a photon's phase (Et − px)/ħ is constant
along the edge generators — the wave that pins the ruler does not
wind along it; (ii) a resting mass winds purely in time at the
Compton rate mc²/ħ, its Compton length ħ/(mc) is the same winding
read spatially, and the ratio of the two windings is exactly c —
every mass carries a private copy of the edge slope; (iii)
l_P/t_P = c identically for any G: G scales the Planck cell, it
cannot tilt the edge.

## 3. Power closes the v14.620 extension
Joules per second — Jeremy's extension of the unit circle "to other
actions and power," left open at v14.620 — is the first unit that
needs all three pins: a watt is a winding rate (circle) of an
edge-converted energy (edge) and the only power constructible from
G is the Planck power c⁵/G = 3.62825e52 W (recorded 2026-10-19).
The edge and the circle together cannot build a watt; power is
where the unpinned pin first becomes unavoidable in the unit
system itself.

## 4. Acceleration: presence as shape, null as response
(Jeremy's correction, 2026-10-31: "constant acceleration is a
parabola though.") Constant acceleration's address on the cone is
the parabola family itself — the mesh is woven from
constant-acceleration curves, and the (X, T) free-fall worldline
x = (1/2)gt² is a parabola. Against the edge pin its bend has one
scale, c²/g (the Rindler length): for g = 9.8 m/s², c²/g =
9.17e15 m ≈ 0.97 ly. The seat null of the paraboloid construction
is a statement about RESPONSE, not presence: a balanced pair at the
shared focus takes no torque from uniform g, so the uniform part
yields no local measurement; g reaches measurement only through
the pendulum depth β = 2IMgl/ħ² for a chosen rotor (v14.626 —
reads g, never G) and through the gradient channel β₂ (v14.630;
Earth's vertical gradient 2g/R⊕ ≈ 3.08e-6 s^-2). The cone is made
of acceleration and blind to its uniform part; both halves are the
equivalence principle in cone language.

## 5. Remark (Jeremy): acceleration is charge-coupled in general
Recorded as Jeremy's remark, with its three standard instances.
(i) Larmor: an accelerated charge radiates, P ∝ q²a² (with
radiation reaction as the feedback) — acceleration of a charge is
never free. (ii) Unruh: acceleration is priced in BOTH pins,
T = ħa/(2πck_B) (at 9.8 m/s², ≈ 4e-20 K) — an accelerated detector
reads its own a as a temperature set by the circle quantum and the

monopole field moves on a cone whose half-angle is fixed by
s/|J| with s = eg — the MIC material on record (v14.731ff): the
cone of this project appears in physics first as the trajectory of
an accelerated charge, its opening set by the charge product.
