## The statement

puts each integer at a point zₙ with |zₙ| = 1; the diameter–secant
machine is chords of a circle; the cone is the circle lifted into

**ħ is the unit circle read in action units.**

## The exact anchor: quantization pattern = AM/GM splits

The mechanism claim is about the *pattern* of quantization — the
halves and quarters — and it is exact arithmetic. Take the
diameter–secant split into consecutive segments (n, k) = (j+1, j):

- AM = j + ½, GM = √(j(j+1)), offset = ½ (identically, for all j);
- the machine's identity AM² − offset² = GM² reads
  (j+½)² − ¼ = j(j+1).

So the rotor Casimir j(j+1) is GM² of the consecutive split, the
Langer (j+½)² is AM², and the universal ¼ common to the Casimir
correction, the Maslov index, and the bouncer comb (n − ¼)^{2/3}

integers. The AM–GM ratio itself is in-family:
AM/GM = cosh ψ_{n,k} ≥ 1, equality iff n = k. ħ appears in none of
these formulas: it is the conversion factor between the pure-number

the lattice is read as action. The chain of the foundation reads:
unit circle → diameter splits → AM/GM → the spectral halves and
quarters.
