# AM–GM unit-circle seat to bicone relative Larmor angle: exact conditional exchange curvature

Date 2026-10-25. Parents v14.366–367, v14.767, v14.856, v14.862. Gravity/bicone side lane; no protected Suzuki/far-tail edits.

[D] At fixed T=u and X=δ, |δ|<u, the cone relation gives Y=sqrt(u²-δ²). Two mirror unit-circle contact directions are z_±=±δ/u + i sqrt(1-(δ/u)²). Their (unsigned) separation is θ=2 arcsin(|δ|/u), so cos θ=1-2(δ/u)² exactly. The opposed fixed-factor parabolas Y²=u²±2uX intersect at (0,u), but this angle construction moves along fixed T, NOT along either fixed-factor parabola. The reflection pairing of points is a specified symmetry choice.

[I] Introduce an explicit **orientation lock**: classical equal-length Larmor/angular-momentum vectors J1=j n_+, J2=j n_- with n_± the directions of z_± in a common meridian plane. This lock is NOT implied by the unit-circle contact geometry, by the pre-existing magnetic driver, or by SO(4) bicone algebra. Introduce separately the postulated ferromagnetic exchange H_ex=-λ J1·J2, λ>0. Then
\[
\Delta E(\delta)=\lambda j²(1-\cos θ)
=2\lambda j²(\delta/u)²
\]
EXACTLY, at all |δ|<u; k=4λj²/u² in the δ coordinate. In the dimensionless ξ=δ/u coordinate the exact curvature equals 4λj² and is independent of u. If λ,j fixed across u=1 and 1/2, raw coordinate stiffness ratio is 4; it does NOT imply a gravity coupling hierarchy. Note physical spin coherent product states |j,n_+>⊗|j,n_-> have exact exchange expectation -λj² cos θ, but the product is NOT generally an eigenstate of exchange, and its preparation/adiabatic status is independent input.
