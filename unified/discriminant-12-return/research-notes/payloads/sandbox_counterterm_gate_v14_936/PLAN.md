## Operative action (from SEED_FORMULA.md)

S(n) = c*(z_n*V(n) - U(n))/2 + d_n*y_n + sigma*2*p_n*P_tot,
V(n) = sum_{m != n} (h-g)_nm y_m,  U(n) = sum (h+g)_nm (z y)_m,
h = 1/(n-m), g = 1/(n+m);  h-g = 2m/(n^2-m^2), h+g = 2n/(n^2-m^2).
Above-band tails are moment series: -2*sum_j n^{2j} V_j(split),
-2*sum_j n^{2j+1} U_j(split), V_j = sum_m y_m/m^{2j+1},
U_j = sum_m (zy)_m/m^{2j+2}, j = 0..10 + envelope remainders.
Seed drift mechanism (TASK2/TASK2B): the U-channel below-band
smooth sum of y_m = yhat_m/l_m, l_m = ln(m/4), with the nonprime
mean pi/2 of z_m, gives -(W_0/2)*ln ln(n/4) in N*S(N).

## Construction strategy (to test, in order)

(A) Output-supported phantom channel family (primary; matches
    v14.909 sec.3.1 "genuinely output-local realization" and
    TASK4 sec.3(a)/(b)): a NEW affine channel family of the same
    species as the seed's own (w_ph(m) = sum_j W^ph_j m^{-(2j+1)},
    v_ph(m) = sum_j A^ph_j m^{-(2j+2)}, yhat^ph_m = w_ph - v_ph z_m
    (+ sector shift), y^ph_m = yhat^ph_m / l_m), evaluated ONLY
    through the (zV - U) channel sums at far output rows, with NO
    diagonal term of its own (so W_0 is untouched by construction)
    and action defined as zero on rows < F0 (output support).
    Its pole-analog contribution (sigma*2*p_n*P^ph_tot) is part of
    its constant term and must be included in the A identity.
    Coefficient targets: leading W^ph_0 = -W_0 gives the U-channel
    drift +beta*ln ln (cancelling the seed's -beta); the remaining
    channel data (higher W^ph_j, A^ph_j) must produce A_sigma and
    D_sigma by derived identities, with a three-band remainder.
    Open legitimacy question to answer head-on in the return: is
    an output-supported channel family an accepted approximant
    component in the frozen framework, or is output support itself
    the row-wise forcing sec.4.7 forbids? The distinction to draw:
    the VALUES at far rows are generated by the channel sums from
    frozen analytic data (not attached per-row), and output
    support mirrors the framework's own band decomposition
    (DM producer inputs never included far trial components).
(B) Exact moment vanishing: phantom inputs supported at m >= F0
    whose V/U moment functionals V_j, U_j vanish for all j, making
    the channel sums identically zero at every row n < F0 without
    an output-support declaration. Suspected obstructed (finite
    support impossible by rational-function degree; infinite
    support needs a Mellin-null density) — if (A) fails, develop
    (B) far enough to state the exact obstruction.
(C) If both fail: the return is the obstruction, stated exactly,
    per gate item 7.
