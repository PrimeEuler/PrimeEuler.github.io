# Finite-strain RMS shear convexity and electrical RMS power analogy

Date 2026-10-26. Parent v14.964. Latest HEAD d6e789c7d680dd39e82bdcc4f22ae50984ec8b7e. All new work gravity only; Suzuki physical quarter correspondence PARKED.

RMS electrical power: for ohmic resistor R_el (constant), instantaneous P=R_el I², average <P>=R_el <I²>=R_el I_RMS²; variance identity <I²>=<I>²+Var(I). Sinusoidal zero-mean I_RMS=I_peak/√2. The K4 elastic J=tr B²/(tr B)²−1/3=Var(λ)/(3A²), with A=(λ1+λ2+λ3)/3 and Q²=A²+Var λ. This matches the *mathematical square averaging* but strain eigenvalue variance represents elastic anisotropy energy density via *an added material coefficient*, not literal electrical energy or an emergent resistance/κ.

Finite shear with det B fixed: B(s)=β diag(e^{2s},e^{−s},e^{−s}), det B=β³. Let t=e^{3s}>0. Then J(s)=(t²+2)/(t+2)²−1/3. J≥0 and J(0)=0, dJ/dt=4(t−1)/(t+2)³. The second derivative along log-principal-strain path is d²J/ds²=36 t (−t²+6t−2)/(t+2)^4. Consequently local positive curvature at s=0 (t=1): J''(0)=36*(3)/81=4/3. Taylor J(s)=(2/3)s²+O(s³), agreeing tr E²/9 where E≈diag(2s,−s,−s). But J''(s)<0 for t>3+√7≈5.64575 or 0<t<3−√7≈0.35425: global convexity fails even at fixed determinant. J remains positive and bounded; t→∞ J→2/3, t→0 J→1/6, so shear penalty saturates in severe anisotropy in this family. η>0 merely rescales along fixed det: U_shear=C b^(2/3)ηJ, and b=β^(3/2) constant, so the finite-deformation elastic potential not globally convex along this family. Nonconvex along a chosen log-strain path is not identical to failure of rank-one convexity or material strong ellipticity, which must be separately tested in deformation gradients and full dynamical perturbations. Local η acoustic bounds remain scoped around isotropy; no assertion of universal finite-strain instability. The harmonic mean does not enter original J and does not cure its saturation without new action choice.
