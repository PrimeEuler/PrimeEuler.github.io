# Scalar pair creation from the bicone vacuum: exact operator gate

Date 2026-10-25. Parents v14.097, v14.680, v14.864–865. Additive gravity/bicone note; Suzuki and far-tail untouched.

Two independent bosonic doublets a_i,b_i, i=1,2, have [a_i,a_j†]=[b_i,b_j†]=δij, cross-family commutators zero. Define N_a=sum a_i†a_i, N_b=sum b_i†b_i and diagonal L_k=(a†σ_k a+b†σ_k b)/2. Glued physical sectors obey N_a=N_b=n-1 and decompose under diagonal SU(2) as j⊗j with j=(n-1)/2. The n=1 sector is the Fock vacuum, n=2 is spin-1/2⊗spin-1/2 = scalar singlet ⊕ vector triplet.

Define scalar pair creator
\[
S†=(a_1† b_2†-a_2† b_1†)/\sqrt2.
\]
Then [N_a-N_b,S†]=0 and [L_k,S†]=0 for k=x,y,z because the antisymmetric epsilon tensor is invariant under SU(2). Its vacuum action is the *normalized* singlet
\[
S†|0\rangle=(|1,0;0,1\rangle-|0,1;1,0\rangle)/\sqrt2=|n=2,L=0,M=0\rangle,
\]
and <S|S†|0>=1. In contrast, the symmetric paired creators a_1†b_1†, (a_1†b_2†+a_2†b_1†)/sqrt2, a_2†b_2† create the normalized L=1 triplet. They are not rotational scalars and furnish an operator-level dipole/vector contrast. In particular, any diagonal SU(2)-invariant pair creator acting on vacuum cannot reach L=1, and diagonal vector creators cannot reach L=0 through a rank-one selection from vacuum.

The operator S† changes each spin label from j=0 to j=1/2 and total occupation from 0 to 2; ordinary within-multiplet J± cannot do this. The new final state has TOTAL spin L=0 but each constituent j=1/2. It is not the n=1 vacuum and is not a j=0 per-factor magnetic transition. Under a↔b interchange, S† changes sign if doublet indices are kept in order; spatial lobe interchange and the physical exchange symmetry of the gluings require independent identification.

Geometry caveat: unit outer magnetic transition parabolas Y²=1±2X share focal point (0,0); n=2 first spin-1/2 edge at (0,1). The Fock-space scalar pair creation is not automatically an operator mapping geometric coordinate (0,0) to (0,1). A representation-to-coordinate intertwiner and a Hamiltonian assigning energies are still needed. The singlet has no automatically propagating Higgs pole, U(1) charge source, or gravitational coupling. Coefficient and existence of S† come from Schwinger algebra, not new postulated interaction.

Suggested audit: directly verify commutators and singlet-triplet basis, normalization, source/target occupation, and physical selection boundaries. No Newton G derived.
