# Bicone spin-zero seat and Higgs identification: symmetry and coupling test

Date 2026-10-25. Parents v14.097, v14.680, v14.862–863. Gravity/hypothesis lane. No Suzuki/far-tail writes.

## I. Representation-theoretic test [D]
For two equal spin-j SU(2) representations V_j⊗V_j, total angular momentum L=J1+J2 has exactly one L=0 multiplet. Its projector is the polynomial
P0=prod_{L=1}^{2j} [L(L+1)-Lhat²]/[L(L+1)]
on V_j⊗V_j, for j>0. Under interchange of the two factors, an |L,M> state has parity (-1)^{2j-L}, so the singlet parity is (-1)^{2j}. For half-integral j it is exchange-ODD; for integral j exchange-EVEN. This distinguishes rotational spin zero from interchange symmetry of the geometric AM-GM seat.

For j=1/2, |S>=(|up down>-|down up>)/sqrt2, |T0>=(|up down>+|down up>)/sqrt2. L_a|S>=0, while A_z|S>=|T0> (dimensionless spins). Thus a uniform magnetic driver H_drive∝L_x,L_y,L_z has zero matrix element from S, whereas a relative/staggered field coupling to A can mix S and triplet. From v14.862 with H_ex=-lambda J1·J2, singlet E_S=3lambda/4 and triplet E_T=-lambda/4, gap |lambda|. For lambda>0 singlet is EXCITED, not ground. Nothing in this representation theory determines a propagating massive scalar field, its mass, or vacuum expectation value.

## II. Gauge/physical test [D/O]
The Standard Model Higgs is an SU(2)_L doublet of hypercharge 1/2 before electroweak symmetry breaking; the observed Higgs boson is a spin-0 excitation of its neutral component, not a two-rotor spin-singlet by definition. It couples to charged matter through electroweak/Yukawa structure, not directly to conserved electric charge as a U(1) monopole source. Gravitational coupling to stress-energy is not limited to Higgs-generated rest mass. A two-spin singlet alone is neutral under total spatial rotations, with no necessary electroweak quantum numbers or distinct propagating scalar kinetic term. Therefore the *identity* bicone singlet = physical Higgs is NOT derived, and equality of geometric seat does not imply a common monopole source law.

A gauge-respecting minimal *candidate* scalar seat extension may take a real SM-singlet s with
L_s=1/2 (partial s)^2 - 1/2 m_s² s² -lambda_s s⁴/4 - eta s (H†H) - (c_gamma/(4Lambda))s F_{mu nu}F^{mu nu} + (xi/2)s²R,
where F denotes low-energy electromagnetic field strength only after electroweak symmetry breaking; the UV electroweak operators must use SU(2)_L×U(1)_Y field-strength invariants. A Z2 symmetry s->-s would forbid eta s(H†H) and s F², absent corresponding symmetry breaking. These couplings and a degree of freedom s are extra inputs, not consequences of the finite-dimensional bicone spin-singlet.
