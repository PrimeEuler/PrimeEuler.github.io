# Electromagnetic self-force and gravitational stress-energy: shared-seat test

Date 2026-10-25. Parents v14.680, v14.863–869. Additive gravity/bicone side lane, no Suzuki/far-tail change.

## Concrete regulator/model [I]
Use two separated finite-radius charge blobs (each characteristic radius a) with charges +q and -q, center separation ℓ=ℓ0+δ(t) > 2a, slow internal motion |δdot|≪c, and long-wavelength ℓ0 Ω/c≪1. The pair is neutral with conserved electric charge. This is an explicitly chosen classical dipole regulator and NOT an operator derived from the bicone.

## Electrostatic test [D]
Up to separation-independent self energies and finite-size corrections absent for nonoverlapping spherically symmetric blobs, U_int(δ) = -q²/[4π ε0(ℓ0+δ)]. Let C=q²/(4π ε0). Then U_int'(0)=C/ℓ0² and U_int''(0)=-2C/ℓ0³=-q²/(2π ε0 ℓ0³). δ=0 is not equilibrium of Coulomb energy alone (nonzero first derivative); an external or internal balancing force must first cancel it, and its curvature must overcome the NEGATIVE Coulomb contribution for stability. Thus monopole charge alone does not yield positive seat stiffness.

## Dynamic radiation test [D with long-wavelength/slow-motion assumptions]
p(t)=q[ℓ0+δ(t)] zhat, pddot=q δddot zhat. Leading electric dipole far-field P_rad=q² δddot²/(6π ε0 c³) (nonnegative). The local Abraham–Lorentz-type dipole radiation reaction for δ is F_rr=τ δ''' where τ=q²/(6π ε0 c³); over bounded or periodic motion ∫F_rr δdot dt=-τ∫δddot² dt plus endpoint τ[δddot δdot], the Schott/near-field term. Thus response includes radiation loss and a reversible boundary energy, not automatically a physical positive mass or spring. A naive local third-order equation has runaway/preacceleration issues, so treat reduction of order, causal finite-size response, or nonlocal retarded kernel before extrapolation.

## Inertial/gravitational sector test [D/I]
Finite electromagnetic field energy of each spherical charge is U_self=q²/(8π ε0 a) for a thin spherical shell. Its energy-equivalent mass U_self/c² requires stresses and full renormalized energy-momentum for any physical inertial mass statement (historical 4/3 issue). The *internal δ-coordinate's kinetic inertia* cannot be identified merely with total U_EM/c²; calculate field momentum/velocity-dependent effective action plus mechanical supports. Gravity minimally couples to total stress-energy of matter, field and supports. A weak-field static change of the isolated system's total mass-energy is ΔM=ΔE_total/c², but internal electrostatic U_int/c² alone is not the total gravitational mass change when constraints/work are included. This is a known gravitational response, not evidence of a NEW gravity monopole generated by the same geometric focal point.

## Decision [D/O]
Electromagnetic internal coordinate definitely admits negative Coulomb curvature and positive radiation power, while its inertial coefficient is regulator/dynamics dependent. No unique geometric map of δ to cone X or magnetic Larmor pair is supplied by Maxwell's equations. Therefore "shared seat as electromagnetic inertia" is presently a structural hypothesis, not established. Proposed next test: specify a charge/current four-density J^μ[X] and stress-energy T^μν[X] induced by bicone angle θ(X), integrate out Maxwell field to quadratic order and compare with covariant stress-energy gravitational response. Obtain kernel K_EM(ω), not arbitrary phenomenological k,m,γ.
