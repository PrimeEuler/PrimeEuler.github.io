# 4V Factor-Cell V4 Fourier Overlay with the Divisor Shell-Defect Field

## Scope

This note records a local/global overlay between two already-established structures in the project:

1. the four-vertex factor cell and its universal quarter shift, and
2. the mod-12 V4 character decomposition of divisor-summatory remainder terms.

The goal is not to identify these carriers. The factor-cell V4 is a local four-corner representation; the mod-12 V4 is the arithmetic unit/character structure of U(12)={1,5,7,11}. The exact common object is the same four-sign character algebra, especially the mixed checkerboard character (+,-,-,+).

## 1. Local four-vertex factor cell

For spacing \(\delta\) and state indices \(p,q\), the four squared transition amplitudes are

\[
F_{00}=K_-^2=\delta^2pq,
\]
\[
F_{10}=J_+^2=\delta^2(p+1)q,
\]
\[
F_{01}=J_-^2=\delta^2p(q+1),
\]
\[
F_{11}=K_+^2=\delta^2(p+1)(q+1).
\]

The integer divisor/factor lattice is the vertex lattice itself; this square is the elementary factor cell whose four consecutive lattice vertices carry these values.

Introduce the V4/Walsh characters on the Boolean square:

\[
\chi_0(a,b)=1,
\quad
\chi_5(a,b)=(-1)^a,
\quad
\chi_7(a,b)=(-1)^b,
\quad
\chi_{11}(a,b)=(-1)^{a+b}.
\]

The labels 1,5,7,11 here are representation labels matching the mod-12 V4 character table. They are not a claim that arbitrary factor-cell vertices literally have those residues mod 12.

Define normalized Fourier coefficients

\[
\widehat F_\chi=\frac14\sum_{a,b\in\{0,1\}}\chi(a,b)F_{ab}.
\]

Direct calculation gives

\[
\boxed{\widehat F_{\chi_0}=Y_c^2},
\]

where

\[
Y_c^2=\delta^2\left(p+\frac12\right)\left(q+\frac12\right).
\]

The two generator channels are

\[
\boxed{\widehat F_{\chi_5}=-\frac{\delta}{2}v_c},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat F_{\chi_7}=-\frac{\delta}{2}u_c},
\]

with

\[
u_c=\delta\left(p+\frac12\right),\qquad
v_c=\delta\left(q+\frac12\right).
\]

The mixed/checkerboard channel is

\[
\widehat F_{\chi_{11}}
=\frac14(F_{00}-F_{10}-F_{01}+F_{11})
=\frac{\delta^2}{4}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{\widehat F_{\chi_{11}}=M_F^2=\frac{\delta^2}{4}}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
K_-^2+K_+^2-J_+^2-J_-^2=\delta^2.
\]

Thus the universal quarter shift is exactly the mixed V4/Walsh Fourier coefficient of the four squared corner amplitudes.

The center cone coordinates are recovered from the linear channels:

\[
\boxed{T_c=-\frac{\widehat F_{\chi_5}+\widehat F_{\chi_7}}{\delta}},
\qquad
\boxed{X_c=\frac{\widehat F_{\chi_5}-\widehat F_{\chi_7}}{\delta}}.
\]

So the complete center geometry is encoded in the four V4 Fourier modes

\[
\boxed{
\left(
Y_c^2,
-\frac{\delta v_c}{2},
-\frac{\delta u_c}{2},
\frac{\delta^2}{4}
\right)}.
\]

## 2. Mod-12 V4 character table

The unit group

\[
U(12)=\{1,5,7,11\}\cong V_4
\]

is generated by 5 and 7:

\[
5^2\equiv7^2\equiv1\pmod{12},
\qquad
5\cdot7\equiv11\pmod{12}.
\]

Using the Boolean labeling

\[
1\leftrightarrow(0,0),\quad
5\leftrightarrow(1,0),\quad
7\leftrightarrow(0,1),\quad
11\leftrightarrow(1,1),
\]

the discriminant-12 character has sign pattern

\[
\boxed{(+,-,-,+)}
\]

and can be written

\[
\chi_{12}(a,b)=(-1)^{a+b}.
\]

This is the same mixed/checkerboard character as the local \(\chi_{11}\) channel above. Earlier project work already established the arithmetic factorization

\[
\chi_{12}=\chi_{-4}\chi_{-3}
\]

and interpreted the natural mod-12 V4 as the biquadratic/Galois structure associated with \(\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt3)\), not as a hidden Farey period-four cycle.

## 3. Global divisor shell-defect field

For the divisor staircase of the shell \(xy=n\), define

\[
q_k=\left\lfloor\frac nk\right\rfloor,
\qquad
r_k=n-kq_k=n\bmod k.
\]

At the staircase vertex \((k,q_k)\),

\[
Y_k^2=kq_k=n-r_k.
\]

Therefore the normalized shell-defect field is

\[
\boxed{
\delta_k(n)
=\left\{\frac nk\right\}
=\frac{r_k}{k}
=\frac{n-Y_k^2}{k}.
}
\]

The ordinary harmonic-floor discrepancy is the trivial-character projection:

\[
\boxed{
E_0(n)
=\sum_{k=1}^n\delta_k(n)
=nH_n-D(n).
}
\]

The existing mod-12 divisor construction is the mixed-character projection

\[
\boxed{
E_{12}(n)
=\sum_{k\le n}\chi_{12}(k)\delta_k(n)
=\sum_{k\le n}\chi_{12}(k)\frac{n-Y_k^2}{k}.
}
\]

Thus the previously studied \(\chi_{12}\)-twisted sawtooth has an exact cone-geometric reading: it is the mod-12 checkerboard projection of the global shell-defect field.

The full V4 family is

\[
E_\chi(n)=\sum_{k\le n}\chi(k)\delta_k(n),
\]

for

\[
\chi\in\{1,\chi_{-4},\chi_{-3},\chi_{12}\}.
\]

This organizes the remainder field into four arithmetic Fourier channels.

## 4. Exact quotient-block compression of every character channel

Let

\[
q=\left\lfloor\frac nk\right\rfloor
\]

be constant on a quotient block

\[
L_q\le k\le R_q.
\]

On that block,

\[
\delta_k(n)=\frac nk-q.
\]

For any character \(\chi\), define

\[
H_\chi(N)=\sum_{k\le N}\frac{\chi(k)}{k},
\qquad
C_\chi(N)=\sum_{k\le N}\chi(k).
\]

Then the block contribution is exactly

\[
\boxed{
E_{\chi,q}(n)
=n\bigl[H_\chi(R_q)-H_\chi(L_q-1)\bigr]
-q\bigl[C_\chi(R_q)-C_\chi(L_q-1)\bigr].
}
\]

Summing over the distinct quotient values \(q\in Q(n)\),

\[
\boxed{
E_\chi(n)=\sum_{q\in Q(n)}E_{\chi,q}(n).
}
\]

Since \(|Q(n)|=O(\sqrt n)\), every V4 channel admits the same hyperbola-method compression as the untwisted divisor discrepancy.

For the trivial character this reduces to

\[
E_0(n)
=\sum_q\left[n\Delta H_q-qm_q\right]
=nH_n-D(n).
\]

For \(\chi_{12}\), the same quotient-block geometry compresses the discriminant-12 twisted shell defect.

## 5. Local/global correspondence

The exact correspondence is structural, not an identification of carriers:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{local factor cell} & \text{global divisor staircase}\\
\hline
F_{ab}=Y_{ab}^2 & \delta_k=(n-Y_k^2)/k\\

\widehat F_{\chi_{11}}=\delta^2/4 & E_{12}(n)=\sum\chi_{12}(k)\delta_k

The two generators 5 and 7 therefore supply a natural shared character basis:

- locally they are the two independent one-bit flips of the factor cell;
- globally they are the two independent mod-12 character directions;
- their product gives the mixed checkerboard channel.

The strongest exact statement is

\[
\boxed{
\text{the four-vertex factor cell carries the same V4 character algebra that organizes the mod-12 divisor twist.}
}
\]

## 6. Guardrails

1. Do not identify the factor-cell vertices with literal residue classes \(1,5,7,11\pmod{12}\) unless a separate arithmetic map is supplied.
2. Do not identify the factor-cell V4 with a Farey/null-diamond period. Earlier audits ruled out the naive period-four discriminant-12 interpretation.
3. The common object is the character algebra/sign representation, not the carrier.
4. The local mixed coefficient \(\delta^2/4\) and the global twisted sum \(E_{12}(n)\) are different observables of the same V4 character type.
5. This overlay strengthens the divisor-shell framework but does not alter the exact identities already established for \(nH_n-D(n)\), \(A_n\), quotient blocks, or prime contact count.

## Interpretation

The new synthesis places the recent divisor-shell results into a two-scale Fourier picture.

At one elementary factor cell, the V4 transform separates center product, the two centered linear directions, and the universal mixed quarter shift. Across the full divisor staircase, the same four-character algebra separates the shell-defect field into untwisted and mod-12 twisted channels. Quotient blocks compress all four channels in the same O(\sqrt n) geometry.

This gives the 5/7 generator structure a precise role without making it carry more than is proved.
