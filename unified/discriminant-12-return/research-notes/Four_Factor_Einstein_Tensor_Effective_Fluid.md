# Four-factor Hessian Cone: Einstein tensor and conditional fluid source

Date 2026-10-24. Gravity-only extension of v14.818–820; reserved Suzuki/far-tail untouched. Signature +---, curvature conventions same as v14.820.

Take r=Σlog x_i, three orthonormal shape coordinates z_j, t=(sqrt3/2)r. The factor Hessian metric is
\[
ds^2=e^r(dt^2-\sum_{j=1}^3 dz_j^2),\quad r=2t/\sqrt3.
\]
Prior result: Ric_tt=0, Ric_ij=(2/3)δij, scalar R=-2e^{-r}. Hence
\[
\boxed{G_{tt}=1,\qquad G_{ij}=-\tfrac13\delta_{ij},\qquad G_{ti}=0.}
\]
The coordinate tensor is constant, but the orthonormal-frame tensor falls with e^{-r}:
\[
G_{\hat a\hat b}=e^{-r}\operatorname{diag}(1,-1/3,-1/3,-1/3).
\]

If an independent identification with a physical 3+1 spacetime and Einstein equations G_ab=κ T_ab (Λ=0, κ=8πG_Newton/c⁴ in conventional units) is imposed, its required stress tensor is a perfect fluid with
\[
\rho=e^{-r}/κ,\qquad p=-\rho/3.
\]
Here rho denotes energy density in the chosen units. The ratio w=-1/3 is determined geometrically, but κ and any physical unit conversion are NOT determined: this is a conditional reverse-engineered source rather than a derivation of Newton's constant.

The flat-FLRW interpretation is exact as a mathematical metric. Define cosmic proper-time coordinate τ=√3 e^{r/2}>0 (in dimensionless geometric units). Then
\[
ds^2=dτ^2-(τ^2/3)\sum_j dz_j^2,\quad a(τ)=τ/\sqrt3,\quad H=1/τ.
\]
It is a linearly expanding k_spatial=0 FLRW model, not de Sitter and not the standard matter-dominated solution. Energy conservation for w=-1/3 gives rho proportional to a^{-2}, matching e^{-r}. In an orthonormal frame ρ>0, ρ+p=2ρ/3>0, ρ-|p|=2ρ/3>0, ρ+3p=0: null, weak, and dominant energy conditions hold, strong condition is saturated. For Λ≠0 the attribution between fluid and vacuum shifts, so the equation of state applies specifically to Λ=0.

Warnings: factor-space coordinates are not yet physically calibrated spacetime events or lengths, and the chosen Hessian normalization is not a derived gravitational coupling. The metric curvature diverges as τ→0 and vanishes as τ→∞, but no cosmological model is established for our universe. No identification with Suzuki/Weil arithmetic carriers, no G derived.
