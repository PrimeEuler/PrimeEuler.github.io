## Background and perfect-fluid obstruction

## General symmetry-preserving elastic completion
Let φ^I (I=1,2,3) be comoving spatial material labels and B^{IJ}=−g^{μν}∂μφ^I ∂νφ^J (signature +---), b=sqrt(det B). Positive B background isotropic B^{IJ}=a^-2 δIJ. The dimensionless internal rotational invariant
J(B)=tr(B²)/(tr B)²−1/3
is nonnegative for positive symmetric B by Cauchy-Schwarz and equals zero iff B proportional to identity. Homogeneous of degree zero under B→s B, invariant under internal O(3) and permutations of the three labels. Crucially dJ vanishes AT isotropy (J is quadratic in traceless anisotropy). Hence a material energy density family
rho(B)=C b^(2/3)[1+η J(B)]
has EXACTLY the same homogeneous isotropic energy density and first metric variation at B=a^-2 I as eta=0; therefore it generates the same p=-rho/3 FLRW background when κC=1, for every eta. Yet d²J is nonzero for traceless strains, so linear perturbation elasticity and shear response depend on eta. η is an unconstrained constitutive parameter from AM-GM radial/homogeneity/permutation symmetry; no uniqueness theorem selects it.
On diagonal B eigenvalues, J=sum_i(λ_i−λbar)²/[3(sum_i λ_i)²] up to straightforward algebra; exact definition is above, and this is nonnegative.

## Conditional standard isotropic-solid acoustic window
For locally isotropic relativistic elasticity, with energy density rho, pressure p, shear modulus μ>=0, inertial enthalpy rho+p>0, the usual principal high-frequency characteristic speeds in c=1 units satisfy
c_T²=μ/(rho+p), c_L²=dp/d rho+4μ/[3(rho+p)].
For p=-rho/3, dp/d rho=-1/3, rho+p=2rho/3, hence c_T²=3μ/(2rho), c_L²=-1/3+2μ/rho.
Nonnegative subluminal acoustic squares give rho/6<=μ<=2rho/3, with strictly positive longitudinal speed for μ>rho/6. This is a conditional acoustic criterion, NOT a verified full coupled gravity-matter hyperbolicity/ghost absence calculation, and NOT a demonstration that arbitrary eta maps to any desired μ with acceptable global energy. Positive stable shear is an extra constitutive input not selected by the pure four-factor Hessian.

## Conclusion
Native homogeneity, index permutation and isotropic background constraints do NOT uniquely select a stable matter action: eta continuously modifies shear while preserving the background.
