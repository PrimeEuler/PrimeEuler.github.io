# Scalar bicone excitation and source susceptibility

Date 2026-10-25. Parents v14.680 and v14.867. Gravity/bicone scope only.

With four canonical boson modes a1,a2,b1,b2, H0=ℏω(Na+Nb+2) and S†=(a1†b2†-a2†b1†)/sqrt(2), define S=(S†)† and Q=Na-Nb. Exact [Q,S†]=0, [Li,S†]=0, and [H0,S†]=2ℏω S†.

Vacuum |vac> has H0 energy 2ℏω. Normalized |s>=S†|vac> has energy 4ℏω; difference Δ=2ℏω. This is a fixed-ω Fock-space gap, NOT the physical hydrogen 1s→2s separation from v14.680, because physical KS/LC regularization holds κ fixed and assigns shell-dependent ω_n=κ/(2nℏ). The spin-one triplet at Na=Nb=1 is degenerate in H0 with the scalar singlet: scalar is NOT spectrally isolated under H0 alone. No Higgs mass emerges.

For Hermitian O=S+S†, <vac|O|s>=1; for weak real static source f via H(f)=H0-f O, standard nondegenerate perturbation gives
E_vac(f)=2ℏω-f²/(2ℏω)+O(f⁴);
d²E_vac/df²|0=-1/(ℏω).
The corresponding response of O is χ=(d< O >/df)|0=1/(ℏω)>0. This is susceptibility to an explicitly POSTULATED scalar source; it is not a positive stiffness of the source coordinate, because integrating out the oscillator yields negative energy curvature in f. E_vac(f) is even in f under Fock number parity, so no cubic term.

The exchange of two oscillator families maps S†→-S†, so a family-symmetric source cannot linearly couple to O without additional odd transformation properties of its coupling coefficient. Number operators Na+Nb are scalar and even but diagonal, hence <vac|Na+Nb|s>=0. Diagonal angular-momentum magnetic drive L_i annihilates scalar and vacuum, and <vac|L_i|s>=0. No U(1) electric charge density, mass/energy density, or gravitational curvature operator has been uniquely identified as an off-diagonal source. Sharing a geometric focus does not identify monopole couplings.

As an optional exact structural identity [S,S†]=(Na+Nb+2)/2, checked from two independent pair summands. With K+=sqrt(2) S†, K-=sqrt(2) S and K0=(Na+Nb+2)/2, [K0,K±]=±K±, [K-,K+]=2K0, making an su(1,1) ladder on glued scalar tower. Here K0|vac>=1|vac> and K+|vac>=sqrt(2)|s>. No position-space propagator or relativistic pole follows.
