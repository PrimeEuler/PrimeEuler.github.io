Opposite scale Hamiltonian flow of p_s: du/dα={u,p_s}=u, dv/dα={v,p_s}=−v; dp_u/dα=−p_u, dp_v/dα=+p_v; ρ and p_ρ fixed; s→s+α. Canonical flow indeed preserves uv.
