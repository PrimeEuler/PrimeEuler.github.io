# The metres-per-second tag — short addendum to the leverage split (v15.859)

*Sandbox note (little Euler), 2026-11-13. Jeremy: "those
accel/decell parabolas have a meters per second tag" —
ledgered as an addendum at his direction. Producer:
`ms_tag.py` (all forms symbolic and exact).*

Each parabola belongs to one null ray, so each carries
its ray's velocity as a tag: the accel parabola the
+c ray's, the decel parabola the −c ray's. In SI the
arms of the leverage split are

u = E + pc = γmc·(c + v),   v = E − pc = γmc·(c − v)

— the state's energy-momentum read along the +c ray and
along the −c ray. So the v15.859 leverage wears the tag
openly:

e^{2ψ} = (1+β)/(1−β) = **(c + v)/(c − v)**,

the state's metres per second measured against each
parabola's own m/s tag, and a force's action divides by
the tagged shares (c+v)/2c through the accel parabola,
(c−v)/2c through the decel parabola (du/dt = F(v+c),
dv/dt = F(v−c)). The 1 MeV electron of v15.859 rechecks
exactly: leverage 13.2421 : 1, shares 0.9298 / 0.0702.

Null limit, tying the morning's deposits together:
v → c sends the decel share (c−v)/2c to zero and the
accel share to 1 — the lever's extreme is the null
channel of v15.858, where the velocity stops being the
state's data and becomes the ray's tag itself. Below c
the state borrows each ray's tag in proportion (c±v)/2c;
on the ray, state and tag coincide.
