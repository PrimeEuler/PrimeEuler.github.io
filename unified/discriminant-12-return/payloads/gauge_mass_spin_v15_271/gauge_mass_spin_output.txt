Thomas bookkeeping: naive rest-frame coefficient g/2 = 1.00116;
  Thomas precession subtracts 1/2; net (g-1)/2 = 0.50116.
  Without the transport correction the spin-orbit splitting is 2x
  too large -- hydrogen's fine structure measures the spin-transport
  holonomy (Wigner rotation along the orbit) directly.
