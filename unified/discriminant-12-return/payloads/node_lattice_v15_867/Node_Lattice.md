# The node-lattice reading of the 1/2

*Sandbox note (little Euler), 2026-11-13. Jeremy: could
the 1/2 of the transition parabola be related to the
sin/cos wave transition from positive to negative —
like defining its nodes? Verified below; producer

## The reading [D]

1. **The sign flip is the half turn.** For the phase
   wave ψ = e^{iθ} on the ħ circle, ψ(θ+π) = −ψ(θ)
   identically — the positive-to-negative transition is
   a phase advance of exactly half a turn, h/2 of
   action. Between consecutive nodes the sign is
   constant (verified interval by interval: +, −, +, −),
   so no transition can occur at finer granularity:
   the half turn is the wave's transition quantum.
2. **The nodes define a half lattice.** In turn units,
   sin's nodes sit at (1/2)ℤ; standing waves inherit
   the spacing (nodes at λ/2, L = nλ/2). The transition
   parabola's offset ½ (v14.620) is this lattice's mesh
   image: consecutive nodes straddle their centre at
   half the step, and a sign-changing step is a half
   step — the transition sits at the half because that
   is where the wave itself changes sign. Per sector
   the straddle is h/2 (EM ½, gravity ¼ = q, v15.866):
   each sector's transition is the half of its own
   step, as the sign flip is the half of the turn.
3. **The components transition at quarters.** cos's
   nodes sit at the odd quarter turns (2k+1)/4; the
   (sin, cos) pair taken together transitions at every
   point of (1/4)ℤ, and the wave's antinodes sit at
   those same odd quarters. [I] Filed as a rhyme, not
   a derivation: the full wave's transitions are
   half-turn (the EM vertex's ½), the components'
   quarter-turn (the gravity vertex's ¼).
4. **The record's echo is exact.** The bouncer comb
   (n − ¼)^{2/3} (v14.592, v15.469): its offset is the
   transitions' phase bookkeeping — hard wall π (a
   forced node) + soft turning point π/2, total 3π/2
   per round trip, and (2π(n−1) + 3π/2)/2π = n − ¼
   identically (Maslov index μ = 3). The comb's
   quarter is node counting, not a fitted shift.

## Scope

This explains the *naturalness* of the transition step
— a sign cannot change at finer granularity than half
a period, so the transition structure of anything
built on the circle inherits the half. It does not set
the sector step h (the weld dictionary's datum,
v15.866) and selects nothing: it is the wave-side
reason the halves and quarters of the record
(v14.620's pattern) are halves and quarters.
