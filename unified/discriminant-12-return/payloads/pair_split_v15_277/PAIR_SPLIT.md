# The Pair-Split principle — refined form, as tested

Producers: `pair_split.py` (instances), `pair_split_test.py`
(out-of-sample tests) + outputs.

**Statement (refined).** Each opposed pair on record carries its
own exchange involution (grid swap u ↔ v; seat swap A ↔ B;
retarded ↔ advanced; wedge swap). The involution Z₂-grades the
observable algebra:

- **ODD channel — the generators and the irreversible forces:**
  momentum (offset), angular momentum, rapidity, the paired
  charge (seat half-split εl = s/2), net curvature
  (drive − reaction), radiation reaction (ret − adv)/2, damping.
- **EVEN channel — the invariants as even functions of the
  generators:** the Casimirs and labels (uv = m², L², A²), the
  energy (AM), the shell, equilibrium data. (Wigner form: (m², J²)
  are the even Casimirs of the odd Poincaré generators.)
- **The phase is the exchanged doublet** (e^{+iφ}, e^{−iφ}): the
  state itself is the pair; neither even nor odd.
- **The fixed locus (exchange-invariant states) is balance** —
  one condition read as rest (mechanics), drive = reaction
  (dynamics), detailed balance (statistics), and the vacuum
  (QFT: the exchange-symmetric two-wedge state, whose one-sided
  reduction is exactly thermal).

**Tests.** (1) Schwinger SO(4), out of sample: under A ↔ B, all
24 generator commutators respect parity multiplication
([e,e]⊂e, [e,o]⊂o, [o,o]⊂e) — the exchange is a Cartan-type
algebra automorphism. (2) Kepler audit: the NAIVE form ("labels
are even") FAILS on L (conserved, drift 3.6e-14, and T-odd); the
refined Casimir form holds exactly — A² = 1 + 2EL² to 4.4e-16
along an integrated eccentric orbit; eccentricity is fixed by
even data alone. (3) Exact damped oscillator: the even (spring)
force's work is the state function −ΔU; ALL dissipation passes
through the odd (damping) force (work = ΔE_mech exactly; closure
1.8e-11). Recorded scoping: the split is stated at force /
amplitude level; product-level quantities (powers, fluxes) are
even as odd×odd.

Status: instances and gradings exact; the unity across systems
is synthesis [I]. Failure condition (standing): an irreversible
force or a Casimir-grade label found in the wrong channel, in a
system on record, in its generator (not product) form.
