intersections of row n and column k (symbolic):
   X = -k/2 + n/2 , Y = -sqrt(k)*sqrt(n)
   X = -k/2 + n/2 , Y = sqrt(k)*sqrt(n)
-> exactly TWO: the crossing ((n-k)/2, +sqrt(nk)) and its mirror
   ((n-k)/2, -sqrt(nk)) -- the two nappes of the cone.

foci: row Y^2 = -2n (X - n/2): vertex (n/2, 0), 4p = -2n,
focus = (n/2 - n/2, 0) = (0, 0).
      col Y^2 = +2k (X + k/2): vertex (-k/2, 0), 4p = +2k,
focus = (-k/2 + k/2, 0) = (0, 0).
-> BOTH families are confocal at the ORIGIN (the apex): the mesh
   is exactly the classical confocal-parabola system that defines
   parabolic coordinates -- which is why the HJ equation separates
   there (MIC v14.731, TN tn_sector.py).
