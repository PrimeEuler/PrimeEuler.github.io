## Link 1: the shell is tangent at its vertex to one circle [D]

In the circle view (X, T) Euclidean, the circle
X² + T² = A² meets the shell T² − X² = g² at
X² = (A²−g²)/2, T² = (A²+g²)/2. For A > g, two mirror
intersections; for A < g, none; at A = g a single point
(0, g) — the vertex — where the gradients of the two
curves coincide ((0, 2g) both): a true tangency, with
common tangent line T = g, the E = mc² line. Each shell
is tangent at its seat to exactly one circle, the one
whose radius is the shell's own GM.
