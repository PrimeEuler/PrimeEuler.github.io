## The null channel closes; the massive channel keeps its ψ [D]

On the pair cone, E² − p² = m² with the shell labelled
by GM = m. The bicone's surface is the m = 0 case, and
there **E = |p| identically** — in SI dress E = pc is
the surface equation, not an imported law. In (ct, x)
axes the null ray is the 45° diagonal (the pair plane's
seat direction u = v): c is the slope that converts the
time axis into the space axis, geometry rather than
data. Consequences, exact:

- A photon stream of power P carries momentum at rate
  P/c: F = P/c on absorption, 2P/c on reflection.
  Force from energy data *alone* — no trajectory, no
  family, nothing supplied but P. This is why the
  record's radiation work (drive×reaction, v15.275;
  Larmor/quadrupole fingerprints, v15.470) always
  closed: radiation lives on the null cone.
- The massive contrast in one line: on a shell, v/c =
  tanh ψ and F_∥ = P/(c·tanh ψ) — at fixed power the
  force runs over (P/c, ∞) as ψ runs over (∞, 0). Only
  the null limit ψ → ∞ pins v to the cone's slope and
  closes the bookkeeping. On massive shells the same
  wattage buys any force: the HJ verdict (v15.857) with
  the ψ left in.
