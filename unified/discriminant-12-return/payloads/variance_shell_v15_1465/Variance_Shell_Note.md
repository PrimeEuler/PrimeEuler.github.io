## The reading

**1. Offset² is a variance, literally (V1).** For a
pair {n, k}, AM² − GM² = ((n−k)/2)² — and ((n−k)/2)²
is the two-point variance of the pair. The AM–GM gap
that structures the whole frame is a variance. A
shell is therefore readable as a constant-deviation
surface: the photon cone is where the null condition
holds exactly (deviation 0); a mass shell is where
it fails by m².

**2. Mass shells are cone sections (V3).** The state
point ((n+k)/2, (n−k)/2, √(nk)) lies on the cone
identically, and at fixed product nk = m² it sits at
height Y = m with T² − X² = m²: the mass hyperboloid
is the cone's section at height m. The spectrum is a
stack of sections — photon 0, electron 0.511, muon
105.7, proton 938.3, tau 1776.9 (MeV) — nested inside
the one cone, each asymptotic to it: the gap along a
ray is E − |p| = min(n, k) (V4), closing like
m e^{−ψ} under boost, and m/E = sech ψ (V5), the
Gudermannian radius of the unit-circle encoding
(v14.620).

**3. The sectors have elementary variances (V2, V7).**
The first unbalanced EM pair (2,1) has variance 1/4;
the first unbalanced gravity pair (1, 1/2), at the
weld's half-step, has variance 1/16. These are the
numbers the record already carries as spectral
fingerprints: offset² = 1/4 (EM) vs 1/16 (G) in
v15.866, and the quarter-grid Casimir residues

(v15.544 §4). In this reading those residues are

**4. The graviton's placement, with its status.**
