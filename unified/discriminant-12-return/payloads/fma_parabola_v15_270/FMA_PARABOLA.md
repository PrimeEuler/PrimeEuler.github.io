# The acceleration parabolas in F = ma; rest mass at the boost hyperbola's vertex

Producer: `fma_parabola.py` / `fma_parabola_output.txt`.

Constant force F on mass m, starting from rest:
- Newton: x_N = (1/2)(F/m) t² — the acceleration parabola itself,
  labelled by a = F/m. The parabolic mesh is the atlas of all
  constant-force motions; the geometry speaks acceleration
  (dynamics per unit mass), and mass enters only as the exchange
  rate between a parabola's curvature and a force.
- Relativity: p = Ft exactly, and the worldline is the hyperbola
  (x + c²/g)² − c²t² = (c²/g)² with g = F/m. In units u = gt/c,
  x_R = (c²/g)(√(1+u²) − 1) = (c²/g)(u²/2 − u⁴/8 + u⁶/16 − …):
  the F = ma parabola osculates this hyperbola at the rest point —
  agreement through u² (position, velocity, acceleration F/m),
  first disagreement at u⁴ with gap (c²/g)(u⁴/8 − u⁶/16 + …),
  the v15.233 tangency series verbatim. The parabola lies outside:
  Newton overshoots (constant coordinate acceleration is the
  γ → 1 shadow; under constant force the coordinate acceleration
  falls as g/γ³, and the hyperbola bends to the null asymptote).
- Rest mass: the boost hyperbola in momentum space is the mass
  shell E² − p²c² = (mc²)². Its vertex (p = 0, E = mc²) is the
  seat — grid reading: the diagonal point ψ = 0 with GM = s the
  mass label, AM = E, offset = p (v15.234 §B). The tangent at the
  vertex is E = mc² (dE/dp = v = 0). Force slides the point along
  its shell at dp/dt = F without changing the shell: mass picks
  the hyperbola, force picks the pace, and the parabola–hyperbola
  tangency point is exactly that rest vertex.
